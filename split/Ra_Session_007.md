# § Ra Session_007


January 25, 1981
1981年1月25日

## (7.0)

(7.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and the light of our Infinite Creator. I communicate now.
(7.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在我们无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我现在开始通讯。

## (7.1)

(7.1) Questioner You mentioned that you were a member of the Confederation of Planets. What avenues of service, or types of service, are available to members of the Confederation? Would you describe some of them?
(7.1) 发问者 你提到你是星际联邦的成员。联邦成员可用的服务途径或服务类型有哪些？你能描述其中一些吗？

Ra I am Ra. I am assuming that you intend the service which we of the Confederation can offer, rather than the service which is available to our use.
Ra 我是 Ra。我假设你意指我们联邦能够提供的服务，而非可供我们使用的服务。

The service available for our offering to those who call us is equivalent to the square of the distortion/need of that calling divided by, or integrated with, the basic Law of One in its distortion indicating the free will of those who are not aware of the unity of creation.
我们可供向那些呼唤我们者提供的服务，等同于该呼唤的变貌/需求的平方，除以——或整合于——那在其变貌中指涉那些未觉察造物一体性者之自由意志的一的法则之基本形式。

## (7.2)

(7.2) Questioner From this I am assuming that the difficulty you have contacting this planet at this time is the mixture of people here—some being aware of the unity, some not, and for this reason you cannot come openly or give any proof of your contact. Is this correct?
(7.2) 发问者 据此我推测，你们此时接触这个星球的困难之处在于这里的人群混杂——有些人觉察到一体性，有些人没有，因此你们无法公开到来或给出你们接触的任何证据。这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. As we just repeated through this instrument, we must integrate all of the portions of your social memory complex in its illusory disintegration form. Then the product of this can be seen as the limit of our ability to serve.
Ra 我是 Ra。正如我们刚刚通过这个器皿重复的，我们必须整合你们社会记忆复合体在其幻象解体形式中的所有部分。然后其产物可被视为我们服务能力的限度。

We are fortunate that the Law of Service squares the desires of those who call. Otherwise, we would have no beingness in this time/space at this present continuum of the illusion. In short, you are basically correct. The thought of not being able is not a part of our basic thought-form complex towards your peoples, but rather is a maximal consideration of what is possible.
我们很幸运，服务法则对呼唤者的渴望进行了平方运算。否则，在这个幻象的当前连续体中，我们在此时间/空间中将没有存在性。简而言之，你基本上是正确的。"不能"的想法并非我们对待你们人类的基本思想形态复合体的一部分，而是对可能之事的最大化考量。

## (7.3)

(7.3) Questioner By squared, do you mean that if ten people call you can count that, when comparing it to the planetary ratio of people, as 100 people, squaring ten, getting 100? Is that correct?
(7.3) 发问者 你说的平方，是指如果有十个人呼唤，在与行星人口比例比较时，可以将其计为一百人，即十的平方得到一百？这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This is incorrect. The square is sequential — one, two, three, four, each squared by the next number.
Ra 我是 Ra。这是不正确的。平方是连续的——一、二、三、四，每个数被下一个数平方。

## (7.4)

(7.4) Questioner [Inaudible] use an example. If ten, only ten, entities on Earth required your services, how would you compute their call using this square rule?
(7.4) 发问者 [听不清] 举个例子。如果地球上有十个——只有十个——实体需要你们的服务，你们如何使用这个平方规则计算他们的呼唤？

Ra We would square one ten sequential times, raising the number to the tenth square.
Ra 我们将一连续平方十次，将该数字提升至第十次平方。

## (7.5)

(7.5) Questioner What would be the result of this calculation?
(7.5) 发问者 这个计算结果会是多少？

Ra [24-second pause] The result is difficult to transmit. It is one thousand and twelve [1,012], approximately. The entities who call are sometimes not totally unified in their calling, and thus the squaring is slightly less. Thus, there is a statistical loss over a period of call. However, perhaps you may see by this statistically corrected information the squaring mechanism.
Ra [24秒停顿] 结果难以传递。大约是一千零十二[1,012]。呼唤的实体有时并未完全统一于他们的呼唤，因此平方结果略少。因此，在一段呼唤期间内存在统计损失。然而，或许你可以通过这个统计修正后的信息看到平方机制。

## (7.6)

(7.6) Questioner About how many entities at present are calling from planet Earth for your services?
(7.6) 发问者 目前大约有多少实体从行星地球呼唤你们的服务？

Ra I am called personally by three hundred fifty-two thousand [352,000]. The Confederation, in its entire spectrum of entity-complexes, is called by six hundred thirty-two millions [632,000,000] of your mind/body/spirit complexes. These numbers have been simplified.
Ra 我被三十五万两千[352,000]个实体以个人方式呼唤。联邦在其全部实体复合体范围内，被六亿三千两百万[632,000,000]个你们的心/身/灵复合体呼唤。这些数字已经简化。

## (7.7)

(7.7) Questioner Can you tell me what the result of the application of the Law of Squares is to those figures?
(7.7) 发问者 你能告诉我将平方法则应用于这些数字的结果是什么吗？

Ra The number is approximately meaningless in the finite sense as there are many, many digits. It, however, constitutes a great calling which we of all creation feel and hear as if our own entities were distorted towards a great and overwhelming sorrow. It demands our service.
Ra 这个数字在有限意义上几乎毫无意义，因为它有许多许多位数。然而，它构成了一次伟大的呼唤，我们这些属于所有造物的存在都感受到并听到，仿佛我们自己的实体都朝向一种巨大而压倒性的悲伤而变貌。它要求我们的服务。

## (7.8)

(7.8) Questioner At what point would this calling be enough for you to openly come among the people on Earth? How many entities on Earth would have to call the Confederation?
(7.8) 发问者 在什么时刻，这种呼唤足以让你们公开来到地球人类中间？地球上需要有多少实体呼唤联邦？

Ra I am Ra. We do not calculate the possibility of coming among your peoples by the numbers of calling, but by a consensus among an entire societal-memory complex which has become aware of the infinite consciousness of all things. This has been possible among your peoples only in isolated instances.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们计算来到你们人类中间的可能性，不是通过呼唤的数量，而是通过一个已经觉察到万物无限意识的整个社会记忆复合体之间的共识。这在你们人类中只在孤立的个案中成为可能。

In the case wherein a social memory complex which is servant of the Creator sees this situation and has an idea for the appropriate aid which can only be done among your peoples, the social memory complex desiring this project lays it before the Council of Saturn. If it is approved, quarantine is lifted.
在如下情况中——当一个作为造物主仆人的社会记忆复合体看到这种情况，并有一个关于只能在你们人类中间进行的适当援助的想法时，渴望这个计划的社会记忆复合体将其提交给土星议会。如果获得批准，隔离将被解除。

## (7.9)

(7.9) Questioner I have a question here, I believe, about that Council from Jim. Who are the members, and how does the Council function?
(7.9) 发问者 我这里有一个关于那个议会的问题，我想是来自Jim的。成员是谁，议会如何运作？

Ra I am Ra. The members of the Council are representatives from the Confederation and from those vibratory levels of your inner planes bearing responsibility for your third density. The names are not important because there are no names. Your mind/body/spirit complexes request names, and so, in many cases, the vibratory sound complexes which are consonant with the vibratory distortions of each entity are used. However, the name concept is not part of the Council. If names are requested, we will attempt them. However, not all have chosen names.
Ra 我是 Ra。议会的成员是来自联邦的代表，以及来自你们内在层面对你们第三密度负有责任的振动层级的代表。名字并不重要，因为不存在名字。你们的心/身/灵复合体要求名字，因此在许多情况下，使用与每个实体振动变貌相协调的声音振动复合体。然而，名字概念并非议会的组成部分。如果要求名字，我们将尝试提供。然而，并非所有成员都选择了名字。

In number, the Council that sits in constant session—though varying in its members by means of balancing which takes place, what you would call, irregularly—is nine. That is the Session Council. To back up this Council, there are twenty-four entities which offer their services as requested. These entities faithfully watch and have been called the Guardians.
从数量上说，持续开会的议会——尽管其成员通过你们所称的不定期发生的平衡而变动——是九位。这就是会议议会。为支持这个议会，有二十四个实体按要求提供他们的服务。这些实体忠实地守望，被称为守护者。

The Council operates by means of, what you would call, telepathic contact with the oneness or unity of the nine, the distortions blending harmoniously so that the Law of One prevails with ease. When a need for thought is present, the Council retains the distortion-complex of this need, balancing it as described, and then recommends what it considers as appropriate action. This includes: one, the duty of admitting social memory complexes to the Confederation; two, offering aid to those who are unsure how to aid the social memory complex requesting aid in a way consonant with both the call, the Law, and the number of those calling (that is to say, sometimes the resistance of the call); three, internal questions in the Council are determined.
议会通过你们所称的心灵感应接触来运作，与九位成员的一体性或统一性进行沟通，其变貌和谐地融合，使一的法则得以轻松运行。当需要思考时，议会保留该需求的变貌复合体，按所述方式平衡它，然后推荐其认为适当的行动。这包括：一，接纳社会记忆复合体加入联邦的职责；二，向那些不确定如何以符合呼唤、法则及呼唤者数量（也就是说，有时对于呼唤的阻力）的方式援助请求援助的社会记忆复合体提供援助；三，决定议会内部的问题。

These are the prominent duties of the Council. They are, if in any doubt, able to contact the twenty-four who then offer consensus judgment/thinking to the Council. The Council then may reconsider any question.
这些是议会的主要职责。如有任何疑问，他们能够联系那二十四位，后者随后向议会提供共识判断/思考。议会然后可以重新考虑任何问题。

我们被允许提供的事件的奥秘和未知特质，具有使你们人类觉察到无限可能性的期望意图。当你们人类领会无限时，那时，也唯有那时，通往一的法则的门户才能被打开。
