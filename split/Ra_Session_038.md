# § Ra Session_038


March 13, 1981

1981年3月13日

## (38.0)

(38.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.

(38.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (38.1)

(38.1) Questioner Will you tell us if there would be any hope or any purpose in either Jim or I taking the instrument's place at the beginning of the session and attempting to replace the instrument as instrument by attempting the trance work ourselves?

(38.1) 发问者 你能否告诉我们，如果 Jim 或我在集会开始时替代器皿的位置，尝试自己进行出神工作来作为器皿，是否有任何希望或意义？

Ra I am Ra. This information is on the borderline of infringement upon free will. We shall, however, assume your desire to constitute permission to speak slightly beyond limits set by Confederation guidelines, shall we say.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个信息处于侵犯自由意志的边界线上。然而，我们将假定你们的渴望构成了许可，允许我们稍微超出星际联邦指导方针所设定的界限，容我们说。

At this space/time nexus neither the one known as Don nor the one known as Jim is available for this working. The one known as Don, by, shall we say, practicing the mechanics of contact and service to others by means of the channeling, as you call it, would in a certain length of your time become able to do this working. The one known as Jim would find it difficult to become a channel of this type without more practice also over a longer period of time. Then we should have to experiment with the harmonics developed by this practice. This is true in both cases.

在这个空间/时间连接点上，无论被称为 Don 的实体还是被称为 Jim 的实体，目前都无法进行这项工作。被称为 Don 的实体，通过——容我们说——练习接触的机制以及通过你们所谓的管道方式来服务他人，在你们时间的某一长度之后将能够进行这项工作。被称为 Jim 的实体则会发现，如果没有更多练习以及更长时间，很难成为这种类型的管道。然后我们将不得不实验由这种练习发展出的谐波。这两种情况都是如此。

## (38.2)

(38.2) Questioner Thank you. Backtracking just a little bit today, I would like to know if the reason the nuclear energy was brought into this density forty or so years ago had anything to do with giving the entities that were here, who had caused the destruction of Maldek, another chance to use nuclear energy peacefully rather than destructively? Is this correct?

(38.2) 发问者 谢谢你。今天稍微回溯一下，我想知道大约四十年前核能被带入这个密度的原因，是否与给予那些在此处、曾导致马尔德克毁灭的实体们另一个机会，以和平而非破坏性的方式使用核能有关？这是正确的吗？

Ra I am Ra. This is incorrect in that it places cart before horse, as your people say. The desire for this type of information attracted this data to your people. It was not given for a reason from outside influences; rather, it was desired by your peoples. From this point forward your reasoning is correct in that entities had desired the second chance which you mentioned.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是不正确的，因为它本末倒置了，用你们的话说。对这类信息的渴望将这一数据吸引到了你们人群之中。它并非出于外部影响的理由而被给予；相反，它是被你们人群所渴望的。从这一点往后，你的推理是正确的，即实体们渴望了你所提到的第二次机会。

## (38.3)

(38.3) Questioner What was the mechanism of fulfilling the desire for the information regarding nuclear energy?

(38.3) 发问者 满足对核能信息渴望的机制是什么？

Ra I am Ra. As we understand your query, the mechanism was what you may call inspiration.

Ra 我是 Ra。按照我们对你的询问的理解，这个机制是你们可以称之为灵感的东西。

## (38.4)

(38.4) Questioner Was this inspiration a— Would an entity impress the person desiring the information with thoughts? Would this be the mechanism of inspiration?

(38.4) 发问者 这种灵感是一种——是否一个实体会将思想印刻在渴望该信息的人身上？这是否就是灵感的机制？

Ra I am Ra. The mechanism of inspiration involves an extraordinary faculty of desire, or will, to know or to receive in a certain area accompanied by the ability to open to and trust in what you may call intuition.

Ra 我是 Ra。灵感的机制涉及一种非凡的渴望或意志能力，即在某个领域去知晓或接收，并伴随着向你们可称之为直觉的东西敞开并信任的能力。

## (38.5)

(38.5) Questioner Could you tell me how each of the rays, red through violet, would appear in a perfectly balanced, undistorted entity?

(38.5) 发问者 你能否告诉我，从红色到紫罗兰色的每一道光芒，在一个完美平衡、无变貌的实体中会如何显现？

Ra I am Ra. We cannot tell you this for each balance is perfect and each unique. We do not mean to be obscure.

Ra 我是 Ra。我们无法告诉你这一点，因为每一种平衡都是完美的，且每一种都是独特的。我们并非有意含糊其辞。

Let us offer an example. In a particular entity—let us use this instrument—the rays may be viewed as extremely even, red, orange, yellow. The green ray is extremely bright. This is, shall we say, balanced by a dimmer indigo. Between these two the point of balance resides, the blue ray of the communicator sparkling in strength above the ordinary.

让我们提供一个例子。在一个特定的实体中——让我们以这个器皿为例——其光芒可被视为极其均匀：红色、橙色、黄色。绿色光芒极其明亮。这，容我们说，被较暗淡的靛蓝色光芒所平衡。在这两者之间存在着平衡点，沟通者的蓝色光芒以超越寻常的强度闪耀着。

In the violet ray we see this unique spectrograph, if you will, and at the same time the pure violet surrounding the whole. This in turn, surrounded by that which mixes the red and violet ray, indicating the integration of mind, body, and spirit. This surrounded in turn by the vibratory pattern of this entity's true density.

在紫罗兰色光芒中，我们看到这独特的光谱图，如果你愿意这么说的话，同时纯粹的紫罗兰色环绕着整体。这又被混合了红色和紫罗兰色光芒的东西所环绕，指示着心智、身体和灵性的整合。这又被该实体真实密度的振动模式所环绕。

This description may be seen to be both unbalanced and in perfect balance. The latter understanding is extremely helpful in dealing with other-selves. The ability to feel blockages is useful only to the healer. There is not properly a tiny fraction of judgment when viewing a balance in colors. Of course, when we see many of the energy plexi weakened and blocked, we may understand that an entity has not yet grasped the baton and begun the race. However, the potentials are always there. All the rays fully balanced are there in waiting to be activated.

这个描述可以被视为既不平衡又处于完美平衡之中。后一种理解在处理其他自我时极其有帮助。感受阻塞的能力只对治疗者有用。在观照颜色中的平衡时，严格来说不应有丝毫评判。当然，当我们看到许多能量丛被削弱和阻塞时，我们可以理解一个实体尚未握住接力棒并开始赛跑。然而，潜能始终存在。所有完全平衡的光芒都在那里，等待着被激活。

Perhaps another way to address your query is this: In the fully potentiated entity, the rays mount one upon the other with equal vibratory brilliance and scintillating sheen until the surrounding color is white. This is what you may call potentiated balance in third density.

也许回答你询问的另一种方式是这样的：在完全赋能的实体中，光芒彼此叠加，具有同等的振动光辉和闪烁光泽，直到环绕的颜色变为白色。这就是你们可以称之为第三密度中的赋能平衡。

## (38.6)

(38.6) Questioner Is it possible for a third-density planet to form a social memory complex which operates in third density?

(38.6) 发问者 一个第三密度的行星是否有可能形成一个在第三密度中运作的社会记忆复合体？

Ra I am Ra. It is possible only in the latter, or seventh, portion of such a density when entities are harmoniously readying for graduation.

Ra 我是 Ra。这只有在这样一个密度的后期或第七部分才有可能，那时实体们正在和谐地为毕业做准备。

## (38.7)

(38.7) Questioner Could you give me an example of a planet of this nature, both a third-density service-to-others type and a third-density self-service type at this level of . . . of attainment conditions?

(38.7) 发问者 你能否给我一个这种性质的星球例子，既包括第三密度服务他人类型，也包括在这个……这个达成条件水平上的第三密度服务自我类型？

Ra I am Ra. As far as we are aware, there are no negatively oriented third-density social memory complexes. Positively oriented social memory complexes of third density are not unheard of, but quite rare. However, an entity from the star Sirius’ planetary body has approached this planetary body twice. This entity is late third-density and is part of a third-density social memory complex. This has been referred to in the previous material.[58] The social memory complex is properly a fourth-density phenomenon.

Ra 我是 Ra。据我们所知，不存在负面导向的第三密度社会记忆复合体。正面导向的第三密度社会记忆复合体并非闻所未闻，但相当罕见。然而，一个来自天狼星行星体的实体曾两次接近这个行星体。该实体处于第三密度后期，是一个第三密度社会记忆复合体的一部分。这在此前的资料中已被提及。[58] 社会记忆复合体严格来说是一个第四密度的现象。

## (38.8)

(38.8) Questioner I was wondering if that particular social memory complex from the Sirius star evolved from trees?

(38.8) 发问者 我在想，那个来自天狼星的特殊社会记忆复合体是否是从树木进化而来的？

Ra I am Ra. This approaches correctness. Those second-density vegetation forms which graduated into third density upon this planet bearing the name of Dog were close to the tree as you know it.

Ra 我是 Ra。这接近于正确。那些在这个名为"狗"的行星上从第二密度毕业进入第三密度的植被形态，接近于你们所知的树木。

## (38.9)

(38.9) Questioner I was also wondering then if, since action of a bellicose nature is impossible as far as I understand for vegetation, would not they have the advantage as they move into third density from second as to not carrying a racial memory of a bellicose nature and, therefore, develop a more harmonious society and accelerate their evolution in this nature? Is this true?

(38.9) 发问者 那么我也在想，既然据我所知，好战性质的行为对植被来说是不可能的，当它们从第二密度进入第三密度时，它们是否因为不带有好战性质的种族记忆而具有优势，并因此发展出更和谐的社会，加速了它们在这种性质中的进化？这是真的吗？

Ra I am Ra. This is correct. However, to become balanced and begin to polarize properly it is then necessary to investigate movements of all kinds, especially bellicosity.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是正确的。然而，为了变得平衡并开始正确地极化，那时就有必要研究各种运动，尤其是好战性。

## (38.10)

(38.10) Questioner I am assuming, then, that their investigations of bellicosity were primarily the type that they extracted from Hickson's memory rather than warfare among themselves? Is this correct?

(38.10) 发问者 那么我假设，他们对好战性的研究主要是从 Hickson 的记忆中提取的类型，而非他们之间的战争？这是正确的吗？

Ra I am Ra. This is correct. Entities of this heritage would find it nearly impossible to fight. Indeed, their studies of movements of all kinds is their form of meditation due to the fact that their activity is upon the level of what you would call meditation and thus must be balanced, just as your entities need constant moments of meditation to balance your activities.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是正确的。具有这种传承的实体几乎不可能战斗。确实，他们对各种运动的研究就是他们的冥想形式，因为他们的活动处于你们可称之为冥想的层面，因此必须被平衡，正如你们的实体需要持续的冥想时刻来平衡你们的行动一样。

## (38.11)

(38.11) Questioner I believe this is an important point for us in understanding the balancing aspect of meditation since we have here its antithesis in another type of evolution. These entities moved, we are told by Charlie Hickson, without moving their legs. They . . . I am assuming they use a principle that is somewhat similar to the principle of movement of your crystal bells in moving their physical vehicles. Is this correct?

(38.11) 发问者 我相信这对我们理解冥想的平衡面向是一个重要观点，因为我们在这里看到了它在另一种进化类型中的对立面。据 Charlie Hickson 告诉我们的，这些实体移动时并不移动他们的腿。他们……我假设他们使用一种原理，在移动他们的物理载具时，与你们水晶铃铛的移动原理有些相似。这是正确的吗？

Ra I am Ra. This is partially incorrect.

Ra 我是 Ra。这有一部分是不正确的。

## (38.12)

(38.12) Questioner But I am just assuming that they . . . their method of movement is not a function of mechanical leverage such as ours, but a direct function of the mind somehow connected with the magnetic action of a planet. Is this right?

(38.12) 发问者 但我只是假设他们……他们的移动方式不是像我们这样基于机械杠杆的作用，而是心智以某种方式与行星的磁力作用相连的直接功能。这对吗？

Ra I am Ra. This is largely correct. It is an electromagnetic phenomenon which is controlled by thought impulses of a weak electrical nature.

Ra 我是 Ra。这在很大程度上是正确的。它是一种电磁现象，由微弱电性质的思维脉冲所控制。

## (38.14)

(38.14) Questioner All right, I'll just ask this one. I have here that—

(38.14) 发问者 好的，我就问这一个。我这里写着——

Could you give me some idea of what conditions are like on a fourth-density negative, or self-service, planet? Can you do this?

你能否给我一些关于一个第四密度负面或服务自我的星球上状况是什么样的概念？你能做到吗？

Ra I am Ra. The graduation into fourth-density negative is achieved by those beings who have consciously contacted intelligent infinity through the use of red, orange, and yellow rays of energy. Therefore, the planetary conditions of fourth-density negative include the constant alignment and realignment of entities in efforts to form the dominant patterns of combined energy.

Ra 我是 Ra。毕业进入第四密度负面是由那些通过使用红色、橙色和黄色光芒能量有意识地接触智能无限的存有所达成的。因此，第四密度负面的行星状况包括实体们持续不断地结盟与重新结盟，以努力形成组合能量的主导模式。

The early fourth density is one of the most intensive struggle. When the order of authority has been established and all have fought until convinced that each is in the proper placement for power structure, the social memory complex begins. Always the fourth-density effects of telepathy and the transparency of thought are attempted to be used for the sake of those at the apex of the power structure.

早期的第四密度是最激烈的斗争之一。当权威的秩序被建立，且所有人都战斗直到确信每个人都在权力结构中处于适当位置时，社会记忆复合体便开始形成。第四密度的心灵感应和思想透明的效应总是被试图用于权力结构顶端者的利益。

This, as you may see, is often quite damaging to the further polarization of fourth-density negative entities, for the further negative polarization can only come about through group effort. As the fourth-density entities manage to combine, they then polarize through such services to self as those offered by the crusaders of Orion.

正如你们可能看到的，这往往对第四密度负面实体的进一步极化相当有害，因为进一步的负面极化只能通过群体努力来实现。当第四密度实体们设法结合时，他们便通过诸如猎户座十字军所提供的这类服务自我来进行极化。

You may ask more specific questions in the next session of working. Are there any brief queries before we leave this instrument?

你们可以在下一次工作集会中提出更具体的问题。在我们离开这个器皿之前，有任何简短的问题吗？
