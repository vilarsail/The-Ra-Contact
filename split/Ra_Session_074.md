## (74.2)

(74.2) Questioner Before I get to new material, last session there seems to have been a small error that I corrected then having to do with the statement, "no working comes from it but only through it." Was this an error in transmission? Or what caused this problem?

(74.2) 发问者 在我进入新材料之前，上一次集会似乎有一个小错误，我后来纠正了，涉及那句话："没有工作来自它，而只是通过它。"这是传输中的错误吗？还是什么导致了这个问题？

Ra I am Ra. This instrument, while fully open to our narrow-band contact, at times experiences a sudden strengthening of the distortion which you call pain. This weakens the contact momentarily. This type of increased distortion has been occurring in this instrument’s bodily complex with more frequency in the time period which you may term the previous fortnight. Although it is not normally a phenomenon which causes difficulties in transmission, it did so twice in the previous working. Both times it was necessary to correct or rectify the contact.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个器皿，虽然完全开放于我们的窄带接触，但有时会经历你们称为疼痛的变貌的突然加剧。这会暂时削弱接触。这种加剧的变貌在你们可以称为前两周的时间段内，在这个器皿的身体复合体中更频繁地出现。虽然这通常不是导致传输困难的现象，但在上一次工作中发生了两次。两次都需要修正或纠正接触。

## (74.3)

(74.3) Questioner Could you please describe the trance state as I am somewhat confused with respect to how, when in trance, pain can affect the instrument since I was of the opinion that there would be no feeling of pain of the bodily complex in the trance state?

(74.3) 发问者 你能描述一下出神状态吗？我有些困惑，因为在出神状态下，疼痛怎么会影响器皿，因为我原先认为在出神状态下身体复合体不会有疼痛的感觉？

Ra I am Ra. This is correct. The instrument has no awareness of this or other sensations. However, we of Ra use the yellow-ray activated physical complex as a channel through which to speak. As the mind/body/spirit complex of the instrument leaves this physical shell in our keeping it is finely adjusted to our contact.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是正确的。器皿对这个或其他感觉没有觉知。然而，我们 Ra 使用黄色光芒激活的物理复合体作为我们说话的管道。当器皿的心/身/灵复合体将这具物理外壳交由我们保管时，它被精细地调整以适应我们的接触。

However, the distortion which you call pain, when sufficiently severe, mitigates against proper contact, and—when the increased distortion is violent—can cause the tuning of the channel to waver. This tuning must then be corrected which we may do as the instrument offers us this opportunity freely.

然而，你们称为疼痛的变貌，当足够严重时，会阻碍适当的接触，并且——当加剧的变貌剧烈时——会导致管道调谐动摇。然后必须修正这种调谐，我们可以在器皿自愿提供这个机会时进行修正。

## (74.4)

(74.4) Questioner In a previous session there was a question on the archetypical mind that was not fully answered. I would like to continue with the answer to that question. Could you please continue with that, or would it be necessary for me to read the entire question over again?

(74.4) 发问者 在前一次集会中，有一个关于原型心智的问题没有得到完整回答。我想继续那个问题的答案。你能继续回答吗，还是需要我重新读一遍整个问题？

Ra I am Ra. As a general practice it is well to vibrate the query at the same space/time as the answer is desired. However, in this case it is acceptable to us that a note be inserted at this point in your recording of these sound vibratory complexes referring to the location of the query in previous workings.[36]

Ra 我是 Ra。作为一般做法，最好在期望答案的同一空间/时间振动该询问。然而，在这种情况下，我们接受你可以在记录这些声音振动复合体时在此处插入一个注释，指向该询问在前几次工作中的位置。[36]

The query, though thoughtful, is in some degree falling short of the realization of the nature of the archetypical mind. We may not teach/learn for any other to the extent that we become learn/teachers. Therefore, we shall make some general notations upon this interesting subject and allow the questioner to consider and further refine any queries.

该询问虽然深思熟虑，但在某种程度上仍未达到对原型心智本质的认知。我们不能为任何他人教导/学习到我们成为学习/教导者的程度。因此，我们将对这个有趣的主题做一些一般性的注释，并允许发问者考虑并进一步精炼任何询问。

The archetypical mind may be defined as that mind which is peculiar to the Logos of this planetary sphere. Thusly, unlike the great cosmic all-mind, it contains the material which it pleased the Logos to offer as refinements to the great cosmic beingness. The archetypical mind, then, is that which contains all facets which may affect mind or experience.

原型心智可以被定义为这个行星领域的理则所特有的心智。因此，与伟大的宇宙全体心智不同，它包含了理则乐意提供作为对伟大宇宙存在性的精炼的材料。那么，原型心智是包含了所有可能影响心智或体验的面向的心智。

The Magician was named as a significant archetype. However, it was not recognized that this portion of the archetypical mind represents not a portion of the deep subconscious, but the conscious mind and, more especially, the will. The archetype called by some the High Priestess, then, is the corresponding intuitive, or subconscious faculty.

魔法师被命名为一个重要的原型。然而，没有被认识到的是，这部分原型心智并不代表深层潜意识的一部分，而是代表显意识心智，更特别是意志。那么，被一些人称为女祭司的原型，就是对应的直觉或潜意识官能。

Let us observe the entity as it is in relationship to the archetypical mind. You may consider the possibilities of utilizing the correspondences between the mind/body/spirit in microcosm and the archetypical mind/body/spirit closely approaching the Creator.

让我们观察实体与原型心智的关系。你可以考虑利用微观宇宙中的心/身/灵与接近造物主的原型心/身/灵之间的对应关系的可能性。

For instance, in your ritual performed to purify this place you use the term “Ve Geburah.” It is a correct assumption that this is a portion or aspect of the One Infinite Creator. However, there are various correspondences with the archetypical mind which may be more and more refined by the adept. “Ve Geburah” is the correspondence of Michael, of Mars, of the positive, of maleness. “Ve Gedulah” has correspondences to Jupiter, to femaleness, to the negative, to that portion of the Tree of Life concerned with Auriel.

例如，在你为净化这个地方而执行的仪式中，你使用了术语“Ve Geburah”。这是一个正确的假设，即这是太一无限造物主的一部分或面向。然而，原型心智与各种对应关系，可以被行家不断精炼。“Ve Geburah”是迈克尔、火星、正面性、男性性的对应。“Ve Gedulah”与木星、女性性、负面性、生命之树中与Auriel有关的部分有对应关系。

We could go forward with more and more refinements of these two entries into the archetypical mind. We could discuss color correspondences, relationships with other archetypes, and so forth. This is the work of the adept, not the teach/learner.

我们可以继续对这两个进入原型心智的条目进行越来越多的精炼。我们可以讨论颜色对应、与其他原型的关系等等。这是行家的工作，而非教导/学习者的工作。

We may only suggest that there are systems of study which may address themselves to the aspects of the archetypical mind, and it is well to choose one and study carefully. It is more nearly well if the adept go beyond whatever has been written and make such correspondences that the archetype can be called upon at will.

我们只能建议，有一些研究体系可以处理原型心智的各个方面，最好选择一个并仔细研究。如果行家超越任何已写就的内容，并建立这样的对应关系，使原型可以被随意召唤，那就更好了。

(74.6) 发问者 你能纠正我吗？

Ra 我是 Ra。靛蓝中心对行家的工作确实至关重要。然而，无论它多么结晶化，它都不能在任何程度上纠正其他能量中心的不平衡或阻塞。它们必须从红色开始依次向上清理。[37]

Ra 我是 Ra。人格修炼的核心是三重的：

一，认识你自己。二，接纳你自己。三，成为造物主。

第三步是当完成时，使一个人成为万物最谦卑的仆人，人格透明，完全能够认识和接纳其他自我。

In relation to the pursuit of the magical working, the continuing discipline of the personality involves the adept in knowing itself, accepting itself, and thus clearing the path towards the great indigo gateway to the Creator. To become the Creator is to become all that there is. There is, then, no personality in the sense with which the adept begins its learn/teaching. As the consciousness of the indigo ray becomes more crystalline, more work may be done; more may be expressed from intelligent infinity.

就魔法工作的追求而言，持续的人格修炼涉及行家认识自己，接纳自己，从而清理通往造物主的伟大靛蓝门户的道路。成为造物主就是成为一切万有。于是，在行家开始其学习/教导时所具有的那种意义上的人格不复存在。随着靛蓝光芒的意识变得更加结晶，可以做更多的工作；可以从智能无限中表达更多。
