# § Ra Session_052


May 19, 1981

1981年5月19日

## (52.0)

(52.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.

(52.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (52.1)

(52.1) Questioner In the previous session you stated that "the other type of experience is the fourth, fifth, and sixth densities of other galaxies, and some within your own galaxy, which have learned necessary disciplines of personality to view the universe as one being, and, therefore, are able to proceed from locus to locus by thought alone, materializing the necessary craft."

(52.1) 发问者 在上一次集会中，你陈述说"另一种经验类型是其他银河系以及你们自己银河系内某些第四、第五和第六密度的存有，他们已经学会了必要的个性修炼，将宇宙视为一个存有，因此能够仅凭思想从一个地点推进到另一个地点，并物质化必要的飞行器。"

I would like to ask you when you say that “fourth, fifth, and sixth densities of other galaxies, and some within your own galaxy,” are you stating here that more of the entities in other galaxies have developed the abilities of personality than have in this galaxy for this type of, shall I say, travel? I am using the term galaxy with respect to the lenticular shape of 250 billion stars.

我想问你，当你说"其他银河系以及你们自己银河系内的某些第四、第五和第六密度"时，你是否在说其他银河系中发展出个性能力的实体比这个银河系中更多，用于这种——我可以说——旅行？我使用银河系一词指的是包含2500亿颗恒星的透镜形状。

Ra I am Ra. We have once again used a meaning for this term, galaxy, that does not lie within your vocabulary at this time, if you will call it so. We referred to your star system.

Ra 我是 Ra。我们再次使用了这个词语——银河系——的一种含义，这种含义目前不在你们的词汇范围内，如果你愿意这么说的话。我们指的是你们的恒星系统。

It is incorrect to assume that other star systems are more able to manipulate the dimensions than your own. It is merely that there are many other systems besides your own.

假设其他恒星系统比你们自己的系统更能操控维度是不正确的。只是除了你们自己的系统之外还有许多其他系统。

## (52.2)

(52.2) Questioner Thank you. I think that possibly I am on an important point here because it seems to me that the great work in evolution is the discipline of personality, and it seems that we have two types of moving around the universe, one stemming from disciplines of personality, and the other stemming from what you call the slingshot effect. I won’t even get into the sub-light speeds because I don’t consider that too important. And I only consider this material important with respect to the fact that we are investigating discipline of the personality.

(52.2) 发问者 谢谢你。我想我可能触及了一个重要的点，因为在我看来，进化中的伟大工作是人格的修炼，而且似乎我们有两种在宇宙中移动的方式，一种源于人格的修炼，另一种源于你所说的弹弓效应。我甚至不会涉及亚光速，因为我认为那不太重要。我只认为这个材料重要，因为我们在研究人格的修炼。

Does the use of the slingshot effect for travel—is that a what you might call an intellectual, or a left brain, type of involvement of understanding rather than a right brain type?

使用弹弓效应进行旅行——这是否是一种你可以称之为智力或左脑类型的理解参与，而不是右脑类型？

Ra I am Ra. Your perception on this point is extensive. You penetrate the outer teaching. We prefer not to utilize the terminology of right and left brain due to the inaccuracies of this terminology. Some functions are repetitive or redundant in both lobes, and further, to some entities the functions of the right and left are reversed. However, the heart of the query is worth some consideration.

Ra 我是 Ra。你在这个点上的感知是广泛的。你穿透了外在教导。我们更倾向于不使用右脑和左脑的术语，因为这种术语不准确。有些功能在两个脑叶中都是重复或冗余的，而且对某些实体来说，左右脑的功能是相反的。然而，这个问题的核心值得一些考虑。

The technology of which you, as a social complex, are so enamored at this time is but the birthing of the manipulation of the intelligent energy of the sub-Logos which, when carried much further, may evolve into technology capable of using the gravitic effects of which we spoke. We note that this term is not accurate, but there is no closer term.

你们作为一个社会复合体目前如此迷恋的技术，只是对子理则的智能能量进行操控的萌芽，当它被进一步发展时，可能演变成能够使用我们所说的引力效应的技术。我们注意到这个术语并不准确，但没有更接近的术语。

Therefore, the use of technology to manipulate that outside the self is far, far less of an aid to personal evolution than the disciplines of the mind/body/spirit complex resulting in the whole knowledge of the self in the microcosm and macrocosm.

因此，使用技术来操控自我之外的事物，对个人进化的帮助远远远少于心/身/灵复合体的修炼，后者导致在微观宇宙和宏观宇宙中对自我的完整知识。

To the disciplined entity, all things are open and free. The discipline which opens the universes opens also the gateways to evolution. The difference is that of choosing either to hitchhike to a place where beauty may be seen, or to walk, step by step, independent and free in this independence to praise the strength to walk, and the opportunity for the awareness of beauty.

对于有修炼的实体，一切事物都是开放和自由的。打开宇宙的修炼也打开了进化的大门。区别在于选择搭便车去一个可以看到美的地方，还是一步一步地行走，独立而自由，在这种独立中赞美行走的力量，以及觉察美的机会。

The hitchhiker, instead, is distracted by conversation and the vagaries of the road and, dependent upon the whims of others, is concerned to make the appointment in time. The hitchhiker sees the same beauty, but has not prepared itself for the establishment, in the roots of mind, of the experience.

相反，搭便车者被谈话和道路的变幻莫测所分心，并依赖于他人的心血来潮，关心的是按时赴约。搭便车者看到了同样的美，但没有为在心智根源中建立这种体验做好准备。

## (52.3)

(52.3) Questioner I would ask this question in order to understand the mental disciplines and how they evolve. Do fourth-, fifth-, and sixth-density positive, or service-to-others orientated, social memory complexes use both the slingshot and the personality discipline type of effect for travel, or do they use only one?

(52.3) 发问者 我问这个问题是为了理解心智修炼以及它们如何进化。第四、第五和第六密度的正面或服务他人导向的社会记忆复合体，是同时使用弹弓效应和人格修炼类型的效应进行旅行，还是只使用一种？

Ra I am Ra. The positively oriented social memory complex will be attempting to learn the disciplines of mind, body, and spirit. However, there are some which, having the technology available to use intelligent energy forces to accomplish travel, do so while learning the more appropriate disciplines.

Ra 我是 Ra。正面导向的社会记忆复合体将尝试学习心智、身体和灵性的修炼。然而，有些实体虽然拥有使用智能能量力量来完成旅行的技术，但他们在学习更适当的修炼的同时也使用这种技术。

## (52.4)

(52.4) Questioner Then I am assuming in the positively oriented social memory complexes that a much higher percentage of them use the personality disciplines for this travel. Is this correct?

(52.4) 发问者 那么我假设在正面导向的社会记忆复合体中，使用人格修炼进行这种旅行的比例要高得多。这是正确的吗？

Ra I am Ra. This is correct. As positive fifth density moves into sixth there are virtually no entities which any longer use outer technology for travel or communication.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是正确的。当正面第五密度进入第六密度时，几乎没有任何实体再使用外部技术进行旅行或通讯。

## (52.5)

(52.5) Questioner Could you give me the same information on the negatively oriented social memory complexes as to the ratios, how they use the slingshot or other effect, personality disciplines?

(52.5) 发问者 你能给我关于负面导向的社会记忆复合体的相同信息吗？关于比例，他们如何使用弹弓或其他效应、人格修炼？

Ra I am Ra. The fourth-density negative uses the slingshot gravitic light effect, perhaps 80% of its membership being unable to master the disciplines necessary for alternate methods of travel. In fifth-density negative approximately 50% at some point gain the necessary discipline to use thought to accomplish travel. As the sixth density approaches, the negative orientation is thrown into confusion and little travel is attempted. What travel is done is perhaps 73% of light/thought.

Ra 我是 Ra。第四密度负面使用弹弓引力光效应，大约80%的成员无法掌握替代旅行方法所需的修炼。在第五密度负面中，大约50%在某个时候获得了使用思想完成旅行所需的修炼。随着第六密度的临近，负面导向陷入混乱，很少尝试旅行。所进行的旅行中大约73%是光/思想。

## (52.6)

(52.6) Questioner Is there any difference then, at, say, close to the end of fifth density in the disciplines of personality required for this travel between positive and negative orientation, higher fifth density?

(52.6) 发问者 那么，在第五密度接近结束时，正面和负面导向在更高第五密度所需的这种旅行的人格修炼之间是否有任何区别？

Ra I am Ra. There are patent differences between the polarities, but no difference whatsoever in the completion of the knowledge of the self necessary to accomplish this discipline.

Ra 我是 Ra。两极之间有明显差异，但在完成这一修炼所需的自我知识上没有任何区别。

## (52.7)

(52.7) Questioner Am I correct, then, in assuming that discipline of the personality, knowledge of self, and control, shall I say, in strengthening of the will would be what any fifth-density entity would see as those things of importance?

(52.7) 发问者 那么我的假设是否正确：人格的修炼、自我知识，以及——我可以说——通过加强意志来进行控制，将是任何第五密度实体会视为重要的事情？

Ra I am Ra. In actuality these things are of importance in third through early seventh densities. The only correction in nuance that we would make is your use of the word, control. It is paramount that it be understood that it is not desirable or helpful to the growth of the understanding, may we say, of an entity by itself to control thought processes or impulses except where they may result in actions not consonant with the Law of One.

Ra 我是 Ra。实际上，这些事情在第三密度到第七密度早期都是重要的。我们在细微之处唯一要做的修正是你使用了"控制"一词。至关重要的是要理解，一个实体控制自己的思想过程或冲动，除非它们可能导致不符合一的法则的行动，否则对实体自身的理解成长既不可取也无帮助。

Control may seem to be a shortcut to discipline, peace, and illumination. However, this very control potentiates and necessitates the further incarnative experience in order to balance this control, or repression, of that self which is perfect.

控制可能看起来是通往修炼、和平和启明的捷径。然而，这种控制本身赋能并需要进一步的转世经验，以平衡对那个完美自我的这种控制或压抑。

Instead, we appreciate and recommend the use of your second verb in regard to the use of the will. Acceptance of self, forgiveness of self, and the direction of the will: this is the path towards the disciplined personality. Your faculty of will is that which is powerful within you as co-Creator. You cannot ascribe to this faculty too much importance. Thus it must be carefully used and directed in service to others for those upon the positively oriented path.

相反，我们赞赏并推荐使用你关于意志使用的第二个动词。接纳自我，宽恕自我，以及意志的引导：这是通往有修炼人格的道路。你们的意志能力是你们作为合作造物主内在强大的东西。你们赋予这种能力再多的重视也不为过。因此，对于那些走在正面导向道路上的人，它必须被谨慎地使用并导向服务他人。

There is great danger in the use of the will as the personality becomes stronger, for it may be used even subconsciously in ways reducing the polarity of the entity.

随着人格变得更强大，使用意志存在巨大危险，因为它甚至可能被潜意识地以降低实体极性的方式使用。

(52.8) Questioner I sense, possibly, a connection between what you just said and why so many wanderers have selected harvest time on this planet to incarnate. Am I correct? This is a vague notion.

(52.8) 发问者 我感觉到，可能，你刚才所说的与为什么这么多流浪者选择在这个星球上的收割时节投生之间存在联系。我是对的吗？这是一个模糊的想法。

Ra I am Ra. It is correct that, in the chance to remember that which has been lost in the forgetting, there is a nimiety of opportunity for positive polarization.[78] We believe this is the specific thrust of your query. Please ask further if it is not.

Ra 我是 Ra。在有机会忆起在遗忘中丢失的东西时，确实存在大量的正面极化机会。[78]我们相信这是你问题的具体要点。如果不是，请进一步询问。

## (52.9)

(52.9) Questioner Well, I would just include the question as to why time of harvest is selected by so many wanderers as time for incarnation?

(52.9) 发问者 好吧，我想只包括这个问题：为什么收割时节被如此多的流浪者选择为投生的时间？

Ra I am Ra. There are several reasons for incarnation during harvest. They may be divided by the terms self and other-self.

Ra 我是 Ra。在收割期间投生有几个原因。它们可以用自我和他我这两个术语来划分。

The overriding reason for the offering of these Brothers and Sisters of Sorrow in incarnative states is the possibility of aiding other-selves by the lightening of the planetary-consciousness distortions and the probability of offering catalyst to other-selves which will increase the harvest.

这些忧伤的兄弟姐妹们提供投生状态的首要原因是可能通过减轻行星意识变貌来帮助他我，以及可能为其他自我提供催化剂以增加收割。

There are two other reasons for choosing this service which have to do with the self.

选择这项服务还有另外两个与自我有关的原因。

The wanderer, if it remembers and dedicates itself to service, will polarize much more rapidly than is possible in the far more etiolated realms of higher-density catalyst. [79]

流浪者如果忆起并奉献自己于服务，将比在更高密度催化剂那远为贫乏的领域中可能实现的极化速度快得多。[79]

The final reason is within the mind/body/spirit totality or the social memory complex totality which may judge that an entity, or members of a societal entity, can make use of third-density catalyst to recapitulate a learning/teaching which is adjudged to be less than perfectly balanced. This especially applies to those entering into and proceeding through sixth density wherein the balance between compassion and wisdom is perfected.

最后一个原因在心/身/灵全体或社会记忆复合体全体之内，它可能判断一个实体或社会实体的成员可以利用第三密度催化剂来重新总结一个被判定为不够完美平衡的学习/教导。这尤其适用于那些进入并通过第六密度的实体，在其中慈悲与智慧之间的平衡被完善。

## (52.10)

(52.10) Questioner Thank you. Just as something that I am a little inquisitive about, not much importance, but I’d like to make a statement I intuitively see, which may be wrong.

(52.10) 发问者 谢谢你。只是我有点好奇的事情，不太重要，但我想做一个我直觉上看到的陈述，可能是错误的。

You were speaking of the slingshot effect, and that term has puzzled me.

你谈到了弹弓效应，那个术语让我感到困惑。

The only thing I can see is that you must put energy into the craft until it approaches the velocity of light, and this, of course, requires more and more and more energy. The time dilation occurs, and it seems to me that it would be possible to, by moving at 90° to the direction of travel, somehow change this stored energy in its application of direction, or sense, so that you move out of space/time into time/space with a 90° deflection. Then the energy would be taken out in time/space, and you would re-enter space/time at the end of this energy reversal. Am I in any way correct on this?

我唯一能想到的是，你必须将能量输入飞行器直到它接近光速，这当然需要越来越多、越来越多的能量。时间膨胀发生，在我看来，似乎可以通过以与旅行方向成90°的方向移动，以某种方式改变这种储存能量在方向或意义上的应用，这样你就能以90°的偏转从空间/时间移出进入时间/空间。然后能量将在时间/空间中被取出，你将在这种能量反转结束时重新进入空间/时间。我在这方面有任何正确之处吗？

Ra I am Ra. You are quite correct as far as your language may take you and, due to your training, more able than we to express the concept. Our only correction, if you will, would be to suggest that the 90° of which you speak are an angle which may best be understood as a portion of a tesseract.[80]

Ra 我是 Ra。就你的语言所能表达的范围而言，你是相当正确的，而且由于你的训练，你比我们更能表达这个概念。我们唯一的修正，如果你愿意的话，是建议你所说的90°是一个最好被理解为超立方体一部分的角度。[80]

第二等的课程是在冥想和服务中学习/教导的。在某个时刻，心/身/灵复合体被这些中心思想或变貌如此平滑地激活和平衡，以至于你提到的技术变得相当重要。然而，宇宙——其奥秘未被打破——是一体的。始终在造物主中开始和结束，而非在技术中。
