## (89.20)

(89.20) Questioner You stated that each archetype is a concept complex. Would you please define what you mean by that statement?

(89.20) 发问者 你说过每个原型都是一个概念复合体。请你定义一下你这句话的意思？

Ra I am Ra. Upon the face of it such a definition is without merit, being circular. A concept complex is a complex of concepts just as a molecule is a complex structure made up of more than one type of energy nexus or atom. Each atom within a molecule is its unique identity and, by some means, can be removed from the molecule. The molecule of water can, by chemical means, be caused to separate into hydrogen and oxygen. Separately they cannot be construed to equal water. When formed in the molecular structure which exemplifies water the two are irrefragably water.[94]

Ra 我是 Ra。表面上看，这样的定义没有价值，因为是循环定义。概念复合体是一个由概念组成的复合体，就像一个分子是由多种能量结点或原子组成的复合结构。分子中的每个原子都有其独特的身份，并且可以通过某种方式从分子中移除。水分子可以通过化学手段被分解为氢和氧。单独来看，它们不能被视为水。当它们以体现水的分子结构形成时，两者不可撼动地就是水。[94]

Just in this way each archetype has within it several root atoms of organizational being. Separately the overall structure of the complex cannot be seen. Together, the concept complex is irrefragably one thing.

正如这样，每个原型内部都有组织存在的几个根本原子。单独来看，复合体的整体结构无法被看到。合在一起，这个概念复合体不可撼动地就是一个东西。

However, just as it is most useful in grasping the potentials in your physical systems of the constituting nature of water, so in grasping the nature of an archetype it is useful to have a sense of its component concepts.

然而，正如在理解你们物理系统中水的构成性质之潜力时最为有用一样，在理解一个原型的性质时，对其组成概念有所感知也是有帮助的。
