## (101.2)

(101.2) Questioner Thank you. What has caused the swelling in Jim's body, and what can be done to heal it?
(101.2) 发问者 谢谢你。是什么导致了 Jim 身体的肿胀，以及可以做些什么来治愈它？

Ra I am Ra. For the answer to this query we must begin with the consideration of the serpent, signifying wisdom. This symbol has the value of the ease of viewing the two faces of the one who is wise. Positive wisdom adorns the brow, indicating indigo-ray work. Negative wisdom, by which we intend to signify expressions which effectually separate the self from the other-self, may be symbolized by the poison of the fangs. To use that which a mind/body/spirit complex has gained of wisdom for the uses of separation is to invite the fatal bite of that wisdom’s darker side.
Ra 我是 Ra。要回答这个问题，我们必须从对蛇的考量开始，蛇象征智慧。这个符号的价值在于容易看到智慧者的两面。正面智慧装饰额头，表示靛蓝光芒工作。负面智慧——我们用它来表示有效将自我与其他自我分离的表达——可以用毒牙的毒液来象征。将一个心/身/灵复合体已获得的智慧用于分离的目的，就是邀请那智慧黑暗面的致命咬噬。

The entity has a mental/emotional tendency, which has been lessening in distortion for some of your space/time, towards negative wisdom. The entity being already aware of this causes us not to dwell upon this point but merely to specifically draw the boundaries of the metaphysical background for the energizing of a series of bites from one of your second-density species. In this case the bite was no more than that of one of the arachnids, sometimes called the wood spider.
该实体有一种心智/情感倾向，在你们的一些空间/时间中，其变貌一直在减弱，即朝向负面智慧。该实体已经意识到这一点，使我们不必详述这一点，而只是具体描绘一系列来自你们第二密度物种之一咬噬的激活背后的形而上学背景。在这种情况下，咬噬不过是一种蛛形纲动物，有时被称为木蜘蛛的咬噬。

However, it is possible that were enough work done to test the origin of the pathology of the entity, it is within possibility/probability limits that the testing would show the bite of the cottonmouth rather than the bite of the common wood spider.
然而，如果做了足够的工作来测试该实体病理的起源，在可能性/或然率的范围内，测试可能会显示是棉口蛇的咬噬而非普通木蜘蛛的咬噬。

The energizing took its place within the lymphatic system of the entity’s yellow-ray, physical body. Therefore the working continues. There is increasing strain upon the spleen, the supra-renal glands, the renal complex, and some possibility/probability of difficulty with the liver. Further, the lymphatic difficulties have begun to strain the entity’s bronchial system. This is some general information upon what is to be noted as a somewhat efficient working.
激活发生在该实体黄色光芒物理身体的淋巴系统中。因此，这个工作仍在继续。脾脏、肾上腺、肾脏复合体承受着越来越大的压力，并且肝脏出问题的可能性/或然率也存在。此外，淋巴系统的困难已经开始对该实体的支气管系统造成压力。这是一些关于这个可被视为相当有效的工作的一般信息。

The removal of these distortions has several portions:
移除这些变貌有几个部分：

Firstly, it is well to seek the good offices of the one known as Stuart so that harsh chemical means may be taken to reawaken the histaminic reflexes of the entity and to aid in the removal of edema.
首先，最好寻求名为 Stuart 之人的帮助，以便采取强效的化学手段来重新唤醒该实体的组胺反射，并帮助消除水肿。

Secondly, we suggest that which has already begun; that is, the request of the one known now to this group as Bob that this entity may focus its aid upon the metaphysical connections with the yellow-ray body.
其次，我们建议那已经开始的；即，这个小组现在已知名为 Bob 之人的请求，让该实体可以将其援助集中在与黄色光芒身体的形而上连接上。

Thirdly, the entity must take note of its physical vehicle’s need for potassium. The ingesting of the fruit of the banana palm is recommended.
第三，该实体必须注意其物理载具对钾的需要。建议摄入香蕉棕植物的果实。

Fourthly, the links between the swelling of contumely and the apparent present situation is helpful.[130]
第四，傲慢的肿胀与显而易见的当前情况之间的联系是有帮助的。[130]

As always the support of the harmonious group is an aid, as is meditation. It is to be noted that this entity requires some discipline in the meditation which the others of the group do not find necessary in the same manner. Therefore, the entity may continue with its forms of meditation knowing that each in the group supports it entirely, although the instinct to share in the discipline is not always present.
和往常一样，和谐小组的支持是一种帮助，冥想也是。应该注意到，该实体在冥想中需要某种纪律，而小组中其他人并不以同样的方式觉得这是必要的。因此，该实体可以继续其冥想形式，知道小组中每个人都完全支持它，尽管共享纪律的本能并不总是存在。

Each entity has its ways of viewing and learning from the illusion, and each processes catalyst using unique circuitry. Thus all need not be the same to be equal in will and faith.
每个实体都有其观看和从幻象中学习的方式，每个实体使用独特的电路来处理催化剂。因此，所有人不必相同就能在意志和信心上平等。

## (101.3)

(101.3) Questioner Thank you. I will make a statement as to the way I see the action, and I would request Ra’s comment. I see the present position as the Creator knowing Itself presently using the concept of polarization. We seem to accentuate or to produce catalyst to increase the desired polarization, whether the mechanism be random, through what we call the higher self, or through the services of an oppositely polarized entity acting upon us with catalyst.
(101.3) 发问者 谢谢你。我将就我所看到的行动方式发表一个声明，并请求 Ra 的评论。我看到当前的位置是造物主通过极化的概念当下在认知它自己。我们似乎在强调或产生催化剂以增加所期望的极化，无论这种机制是随机的，通过我们所称为高我的，还是通过一个相反极化实体以催化剂作用于我们的服务。

All of these seem to produce the same effect which is more intense polarization in the desired direction once that direction has been definitely chosen. I see catalyst of the second-density insect bite being a function of either, or of any, of the sources of which I have spoken, from random, to augmented through the higher self, or through the oppositely polarized services of those who monitor our activities—all of which have roughly the same ultimate effect. Would Ra comment on my observation?
所有这些似乎都产生相同的效果，一旦确定方向已经明确选择，就会在所期望的方向上产生更强烈的极化。我看到第二密度昆虫咬嘬的催化剂是我所说的任何源头的一个功能，从随机的，到通过高我增强的，或通过那些监控我们活动的相反极化实体的服务——所有这些都大致产生相同的终极效果。Ra 能否评论我的观察？

Ra I am Ra. We find your observations unexceptional and, in the large, correct.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们发现你的观察无可挑剔，并且大体上是正确的。

## (101.4)

(101.4) Questioner In this particular case, which avenue was the one that produced the catalyst of the bite?
(101.4) 发问者 在这个特定案例中，哪个途径产生了咬嘬的催化剂？

Ra I am Ra. The nature of catalyst is such that there is only one source, for the catalyst and experience are further attempts at specificity in dealing with the architecture of the unconscious mind of the self. Therefore, in an incarnational experience the self as Creator, especially the higher self, is the base from which catalyst stands to offer its service to the mind, body, or spirit.
Ra 我是 Ra。催化剂的本质是这样的，只有一个源头，因为催化剂和体验是在处理自我无意识心智的架构时进一步尝试特定化。因此，在一次投生经历中，作为造物主的自我，尤其是高我，是催化剂站立于其上以向心智、身体或灵性提供服务的基础。

In the sense which we feel you intend, the source was the fifth-density negative friend which had noted the gradual falling away of the inharmonious patterns of the distortion called anger/frustration in the entity. The insect was easily led to an attack, and the physical vehicle, which had long-standing allergies and sensitivities, was also easily led into the mechanisms of the failure of lymphatic function and the greatly diminished ability of the immune system to remove from the yellow-ray body that which distorted it.
在我们感觉你所指的意义上，源头是第五密度的负面朋友，它注意到该实体中被称为愤怒/挫败的变貌的不和谐模式正在逐渐消退。昆虫很容易被引导发动攻击，而具有长期过敏和敏感性的物理载具，也很容易被引导进入淋巴功能失败和免疫系统从黄色光芒身体中清除使其变貌之物的能力大幅降低的机制中。

We can comment, not upon the questioner’s physical distortions but upon the indubitable truth of second-density hive creatures; that is, that a hive mentality as a whole can be influenced by one strong metaphysical impulse. Both the instrument and the scribe have the capacity for great distortions toward nonviability, given such an attack by a great number of the stinging insects.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们可以评论，不是关于发问者的物理变貌，而是关于第二密度蜂群生物的不容置疑的真理；即，蜂群心智作为一个整体可以被一个强大的形而上冲动所影响。器皿和抄写员都有能力在面对大量蜜蜂的攻击时产生迈向不可存活性的巨大变貌。

(101.7) 发问者 为亚特兰大机场以南的另一所房子建议的那种性质的清洁对 Oakdale Road 地址是否合适？

Ra 我是 Ra。我们注意到，任何住所，无论之前是良性的（如你所说的那个）还是之前具有恶性特征，都需要盐、水和扫帚的基本清洁。

The placing of salt may be done at the place which is not being sealed [during] the first of the cleansings, and the salt may be requested to act as seal and yet allow the passage of gentle spirits such as yourselves. We suggest that you speak to this substance and name each entity for which permission is needed in order to pass. Let no person pass without permission being asked of the salt. This is the case in the residence of which you speak.
盐可以放置在第一次清洁时没有被封闭的地方，并且可以请求盐作为封印，但允许像你们这样的温和灵魂通过。我们建议你们对这种物质说话，并命名每个需要许可才能通过的实体。不要让任何人在没有向盐请求许可的情况下通过。这就是你所说的住所的情况。

(101.8) 发问者 谢谢你。Ra 能否提供信息，关于我们可以用任何方式向 Greta Woodrew 提供信息，以缓解她当前的肿胀状况？

Ra I am Ra. We may only suggest that the honor of propinquity to light carries with it the Law of Responsibility.[131] The duty to refrain from contumely, discord, and all things which, when unresolved within, make way for workings lies before the instrument of which you speak. This entity may, if it is desired by the scribe, share our comments upon the working of the latter entity.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们只能建议，接近光的荣耀带有责任法则。[131] 抵制傲慢、不和谐以及所有在内在未解决时为工作开路的事物的责任，摆在你所说的器皿面前。如果抄写员希望，这个实体可以分享我们对后者工作的评论。

The entity which is given constant and unremitting approval by those surrounding it suffers from the loss of the mirroring effect of those which reflect truthfully rather than unquestioningly. This is not a suggestion to reinstate judgment but merely a suggestion for all those supporting instruments; that is, support, be harmonious, share in love, joy, and thanksgiving, but find love within truth, for each instrument benefits from this support more than from the total admiration which overcomes discrimination.
被周围人给予持续不断的认可的实体，会因为失去那些真实反映而非盲目接受的人的镜子效应而受苦。这不是建议重新引入判断，而仅仅是对所有支持器皿者的一个建议；即，支持、和谐、分享爱、喜悦和感恩，但在真理中寻找爱，因为每个器皿从这种支持中获益更多，而非从克服分别心的全然仰慕中获益。
