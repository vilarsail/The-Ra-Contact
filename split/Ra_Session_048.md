# § Ra Session_048


April 22, 1981

1981年4月22日

## (48.0)

(48.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.

(48.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (48.1)

(48.1) Questioner Could you tell me of the instrument's condition and if she is improving with time?

(48.1) 发问者 你能告诉我器皿的状况吗？她是否随着时间有所改善？

Ra I am Ra. This instrument's vital energies are improving with time, as you measure it. This instrument's physical energies are less than your previous asking.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个器皿的生命能量正随着时间——以你们的度量——而改善。这个器皿的肉体能量比你上次询问时更少。

## (48.2)

(48.2) Questioner I have a question from the instrument that I will read: "You have suggested several times that sexual energy transfers aid the instrument's vital energy and this contact. It seems that this is not true for all people; that the sexual circuitry and the spiritual circuitry are not the same. Is this instrument an anomaly, or is the positive effect of sexual activity on spiritual energy normal for all third-density beings?"

(48.2) 发问者 我有一个来自器皿的问题，我将朗读："你曾多次建议，性能量转移有助于器皿的生命能量和这个接触。似乎这并非对所有人都成立；性回路和灵性回路并非同一回事。这个器皿是异常现象，还是性活动对灵性能量的正面效果对所有第三密度存有都是正常的？"

Ra I am Ra. This instrument, though not anomalous, is somewhat less distorted towards the separation of mind, body, and spirit than many of your third-density entities. The energies of sexual transfer would, if run through the undeveloped spiritual, electrical, or magnetic complex which you call circuitry, effectually blow out that particular circuit. Contrarily, the full spiritual energies run through bodily complex circuitry will also adversely affect the undeveloped circuit of the bodily complex.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个器皿，尽管并非异常，但比许多你们第三密度的实体更少地偏向于心、身、灵的分离。性能量转移的能量，如果流经你们称为回路的发展不足的灵性、电性或磁性复合体，将有效地烧毁该特定回路。相反，完整的灵性能量流经身体复合体回路，也会对发展不足的身体复合体回路产生不利影响。

Some there are, such as this instrument, who have not, in the particular incarnation, chosen at any time to express sexual energy through the bodily circuitry. Thus from the beginning of such an entity's experience the body and spirit express together in any sexual action. Therefore, to transfer sexual energy for this instrument is to transfer spiritually as well as physically. This instrument's magnetic field, if scrutinized by one sensitive, will show these unusual configurations.

有一些存有，比如这个器皿，在此特定化身中从未选择通过身体回路表达性能量。因此，从这样一个实体经验的开端，身体与灵性在任何性行为中共同表达。因此，对这个器皿来说，性能量转移既是身体上的转移，也是灵性上的转移。这个器皿的磁场，若被敏感者细察，将显示出这些不寻常的配置。

This is not unique to one entity but is common to a reasonable number of entities who, having lost the desire for orange- and green-ray[72] sexual experiences, have strengthened the combined circuitry of spirit, mind, and body to express the totality of beingness in each action. It is for this reason also that the social intercourse and companionship is very beneficial to this instrument, it being sensitive to the more subtle energy transfers.

这并非独属于某一个实体，而是相当数量的实体所共有的——他们已失去对橙色与绿色光芒[72]性体验的渴望，并强化了灵性、心智与身体的联合回路，在每一个行动中表达存在的整体性。正因如此，社会交往与陪伴对这个器皿也非常有益，因为它对更精微的能量转移十分敏感。

## (48.3)

(48.3) Questioner Thank you. If you, Ra, as an individualized entity were incarnate on Earth now with full awareness and memory of what you know now, what would be your objective at this time on Earth as far as activities are concerned?

(48.3) 发问者 谢谢你。如果你，Ra，作为一个个体化实体现在投生在地球上，并拥有你现今所知的全然觉知与记忆，就活动而言，你此时在地球上的目标会是什么？

Ra I am Ra. The query suggests that which has been learned to be impractical. However, were we to again be naïve enough to think that our physical presence was any more effective than that love/light we send your peoples and the treasure of this contact, we would do as we did do. We would be, and we would offer our selves as teach/learners.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个询问暗示了那些已被认识到不切实际的事情。然而，如果我们再次天真到认为我们的物理临在比我们发送给你们人民的爱/光以及这次接触的珍宝更有效，我们就会做我们曾经做过的。我们将存在，并将我们自身奉献为教导/学习者。

(48.4) 发问者 以你现在对我们行星状况和通讯方式等的了解，如果你自己作为一个个体，曾作为流浪者经历了此处的投生过程，并且现在拥有足够的记忆来实现你刚才所说的目标，在我们当前的通讯状态下，你会寻求什么机制来进行教导/学习的过程？

Ra I am Ra. My brother, we perceive you have made certain unspoken connections. We acknowledge these and, for this reason, cannot infringe upon your confusion.

Ra 我是 Ra。我的兄弟，我们感知到你已建立了某些未言明的联系。我们承认这些，并因此不能侵犯你的混淆。

## (48.5)

(48.5) Questioner I was afraid of that.

(48.5) 发问者 我就怕是这样。

My lecture yesterday[73] was attended by only a few. If this had occurred during a UFO flap, as we call them, many more would have attended. But since Orion entities cause the flaps, primarily, what is Orion's reward, shall I say, for visibility in that they actually create greater chances and opportunities for dissemination of information such as mine at this time?

我昨天的演讲[73]只有少数人参加。如果这发生在我们所谓的UFO浪潮期间，会有更多人参加。但既然猎户座实体主要引发了这些浪潮，我想问，猎户座在可见性方面获得了什么回报——他们实际上为像我这样的信息传播创造了更大的机会和可能性？

Ra I am Ra. This assumption is incorrect. The flaps cause many fears among your peoples, many speakings, understandings concerning plots, cover-ups, mutilations, killings, and other negative impressions. Even those supposedly positive reports which gain public awareness speak of doom. You may understand yourself as one who will be in the minority due to the understandings which you wish to share, if we may use that misnomer.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个假设是错误的。浪潮在你们的人民中引发了许多恐惧、许多关于阴谋、掩盖、残害、杀戮和其他负面印象的谈论与理解。即使是那些获得公众关注的所谓正面报道，也谈论着末日。你可以理解自己将属于少数派，因为你想分享的这些理解——如果我们可以用这个不准确的词的话。

We perceive there is a further point we may posit at this time. The audience brought about by Orion-type publicity is not seeded by seniority of vibration to a great extent. The audiences receiving teach/learnings without stimulus from publicity will be more greatly oriented towards illumination. Therefore, forget you the counting.

我们感知到此时我们还可以提出一个进一步的观点。由猎户座类型的宣传所带来的听众，在很大程度上并非由振动资历来筛选。那些在没有宣传刺激下接受教导/学习的听众，将更倾向于朝向启明。因此，忘掉计数吧。

## (48.6)

(48.6) Questioner Thank you. That cleared it up very well. A very important point.

(48.6) 发问者 谢谢你。这解释得非常清楚。一个非常重要的观点。

Can you tell me how positive and negative polarizations in fourth and fifth density are used to cause work in consciousness?

你能告诉我第四和第五密度中的正面与负面极化如何被用来产生意识中的工作吗？

Ra I am Ra. There is very little work in consciousness in fourth and in fifth densities compared to the work done in third density. The work that is accomplished in positive fourth is that work whereby the positive social memory complex, having through slow stages harmoniously integrated itself, goes forth to aid those of less positive orientation which seek their aid. Thus their service is their work.

Ra 我是 Ra。与第三密度中所完成的工作相比，第四和第五密度中意识中的工作非常少。在正面第四密度中完成的工作是：正面的社会记忆复合体通过缓慢阶段和谐地整合自身后，前行去援助那些寻求帮助的、极化程度较低的正面导向存有。因此，他们的服务就是他们的工作。

And through this dynamic between the societal self and the other-self which is the object of love, greater and greater intensities of understanding or compassion are attained. This intensity continues until the appropriate intensity of the light may be welcomed. This is fourth-density harvest.

通过社会性自我与作为爱的对象的其他自我之间的这种动态，越来越高的理解或慈悲的强度得以达成。这种强度持续增长，直到能够迎接适当强度的光。这就是第四密度收割。

Within fourth-density positive there are minor amounts of catalyst of a spiritual- and mental-complex distortion. This occurs during the process of harmonizing to the extent of forming the social memory complex. This causes some small catalyst and work to occur, but the great work of fourth density lies in the contact betwixt the societal self and less polarized other-self.

在正面第四密度中，存在少量灵性和心智复合体变貌的催化剂。这发生在为形成社会记忆复合体而进行和谐化的过程中。这导致一些小型催化剂和工作的发生，但第四密度真正伟大的工作在于社会性自我与极化程度较低的其他自我之间的接触。

In fourth-density negative much work is accomplished during the fighting for position which precedes the period of the social memory complex. There are opportunities to polarize negatively by control of other-selves. During the social memory complex period of fourth-density negative, the situation is the same. The work takes place through the societal reaching out to less polarized other-self in order to aid in negative polarization.

在负面第四密度中，许多工作在社会记忆复合体时期之前的地位争夺中完成。存在通过控制其他自我来进行负面极化的机会。在负面第四密度的社会记忆复合体时期，情况也是如此。工作通过社会性自我向极化程度较低的其他自我伸出援手以帮助负面极化而进行。

In fifth-density positive and negative the concept of work done through a potential difference is not particularly helpful as fifth-density entities are, again, intensifying rather than potentiating.

在第五密度正面和负面中，通过位势差来完成工作的概念并非特别有用，因为第五密度实体再次是在强化而非增强位势。

In positive, the fifth-density complex uses sixth-density teach/learners to study the more illuminated understandings of unity, thus becoming more and more wise. Fifth-density positive social memory complexes often will choose to divide their service to others in two ways: first, the beaming of light to creation; second, the sending of groups to be of aid as instruments of light such as those whom you're familiar with through channels.

在正面中，第五密度复合体利用第六密度的教导/学习者来研习更光明的合一理解，从而变得越来越有智慧。正面第五密度社会记忆复合体常常会选择将他们的服务他人分为两种方式：第一，向造物发送光；第二，派遣团体作为光的器皿提供帮助，例如你们通过管道所熟悉的那些实体。

In fifth-density negative, service to self has become extremely intense and the self has shrunk or compacted so that the dialogues with the teach/learners are used exclusively in order to intensify wisdom. There are very, very few fifth-density negative wanderers for they fear the forgetting. There are very, very few fifth-density Orion members for they do not any longer perceive any virtue in other-selves.[74]

在负面第五密度中，服务自我变得极其强烈，自我已收缩或紧致化，以至于与教导/学习者的对话被专门用于强化智慧。第五密度的负面流浪者非常非常少，因为他们害怕遗忘。第五密度的猎户座成员也非常非常少，因为他们不再感知到其他自我中有任何美德。[74]

## (48.7)

(48.7) Questioner Thank you. I would like to take as an example an entity, at birth, who is roughly high on the seniority list for positive polarization and possible harvestability at the end of this cycle, and follow a full cycle of his experience starting before his incarnation—which body is activated, process of becoming incarnate, the activation of the third-density physical body process as the body moves through this density and is acted upon by catalyst, and then the process of death, and the activation of the various bodies—so that we make a full circuit from a point prior to incarnation back around through incarnation and death and back to that position, you might say, in one cycle of incarnation in this density. (48.7) 发问者 谢谢你。我想以一个实体为例，他在出生时，在正面极化和本周期末可能收割的资历列表中大致排名靠前，并追踪他经验的完整循环——从他投生之前开始：哪个身体被激活，投生的过程，第三密度物质身体在穿越此密度并受到催化剂作用时的激活过程，然后是死亡过程，以及各个身体的激活——这样我们就从投生前的一个点，绕一圈经由投生和死亡，再回到那个位置，可以说，在这个密度的一次投生循环中完成一个完整的回路。你能为我这样做吗？

Ra I am Ra. Your query is most distorted, for it assumes that creations are alike. Each mind/body/spirit complex has its own patterns of activation and its own rhythms of awakening. The important thing for harvest is the harmonious balance between the various energy centers of the mind/body/spirit complex. This is to be noted as of relative import. We grasp the thrust of your query and will make a most general answer stressing the unimportance of such arbitrary generalizations.

Ra 我是 Ra。你的询问是极其扭曲的，因为它假设造物都是相似的。每个心/身/灵复合体都有其自身的激活模式和觉醒节奏。对收割而言重要的是心/身/灵复合体各个能量中心之间的和谐平衡。这一点应被视为相对重要。我们理解你询问的主旨，并将给出一个最笼统的回答，同时强调这种任意概括的不重要性。

The entity, before incarnation, dwells in the appropriate, shall we say, place in time/space. The true-color type of this location will be dependent upon the entity's needs. Those entities, for instance, which, being wanderers, have the green, blue, or indigo true-color core of mind/body/spirit complex will have rested therein.

该实体在投生之前，居住在时间/空间中适当的地方，容我们说。该位置的真实颜色类型将取决于该实体的需要。例如，那些作为流浪者、拥有绿色、蓝色或靛蓝色真实颜色核心心/身/灵复合体的实体，将在那里安住。

Entrance into incarnation requires the investment, or activation, of the indigo-ray, or etheric body, for this is the form maker. The young, or small, physical mind/body/spirit complex has the seven energy centers potentiated before the birthing process. There are also analogs in time/space of these energy centers corresponding to the seven energy centers in each of the seven true-color densities. Thus in the microcosm exists all the experience that is prepared. It is as though the infant contains the universe.

进入投生需要靛蓝色光芒体或以太体的投入或激活，因为这是形态制造者。年幼或微小的物质心/身/灵复合体在出生过程之前就已将七个能量中心潜能化。在时间/空间中，这些能量中心也有对应物，对应于七个真实颜色密度中每一个的七个能量中心。因此，在微观宇宙中存在着所有准备好的经验。仿佛婴儿包含着宇宙。

Blue ray is the ray of free communication with self and with other-self.

高资历实体的激活模式无疑会以一定的速度移动到绿色光芒层级，这是通往原初蓝色的跳板。穿透蓝色原初能量总是有些困难，因为它需要你们人民极其匮乏的东西；那就是诚实。蓝色光芒是与自我和其他自我自由沟通的光芒。

Having accepted that an harvestable or nearly harvestable entity will be working from this green-ray springboard, one may then posit that the experiences in the remainder of the incarnation will be focused upon activation of the primary blue ray of freely given communication; of indigo ray, that of freely shared intelligent energy; and, if possible, moving through this gateway, the penetration of violet-ray intelligent infinity. This may be seen to be manifested by a sense of the consecrate, or hallowed, nature of everyday creations and activities.

接受了一个可收割或近乎可收割的实体将从这绿色光芒跳板出发后，我们可以假定，该化身剩余部分中的经验将聚焦于激活原初蓝色光芒——自由给予的沟通；靛蓝色光芒——自由分享的智能能量；以及如果可能，穿越这道门户，穿透紫罗兰色光芒的智能无限。这可以通过一种对日常造物和活动的神圣或圣化本质的感知来显现。

在身体复合体死亡——你们称为这个过渡——时，实体一旦意识到自身状态，将立即返回靛蓝色形态制造者身体并在其中安住，直到做出适当的未来安置。

Here we have the anomaly of harvest. In harvest the entity will then transfer its indigo body into violet-ray manifestation as seen in true-color yellow. 这里我们有收割的异常之处。在收割中，实体将把其靛蓝色身体转化为在真实颜色黄色中所见的紫罗兰色光芒显化。这是为了衡量该实体的可收割性。在此异常活动被仔细完成后，实体将再次进入靛蓝色身体，并被安置在空间/时间和时间/空间中正确的真实颜色位置，届时必要的疗愈和教导/学习将被完成，进一步的投生需求将被确定。

## (48.11)

(48.11) Questioner Just if there is anything that we can do to make the instrument more comfortable or improve the contact?

(48.11) 发问者 只是想知道我们能否做些什么使器皿更舒适或改善这个接触？

Ra I am Ra. All is well. As we have said, this instrument is weak physically, and continued work times will lengthen this weakness. The continued contact also aids in the continued climb in vital energy of the instrument, as well as the integration and vital energy of the group as an unit. The choice is yours. We are pleased. All is well. You are conscientious. Continue so.

Ra 我是 Ra。一切都好。正如我们所说，这个器皿在身体上是虚弱的，持续的工作时间将延长这种虚弱。持续的接触也有助于器皿生命能量的持续攀升，以及作为整体的团体的整合与生命能量。选择是你们的。我们感到满意。一切都好。你们是尽责的。继续如此。

I am Ra. I leave you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. Go forth, then, my friends, rejoicing in the power and in the peace of the One Infinite Creator. Adonai.

我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中离开你们。那么，向前去吧，我的朋友们，在太一无限造物主的大能与和平中欢欣鼓舞。Adonai。
