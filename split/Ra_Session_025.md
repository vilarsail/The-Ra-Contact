# § Ra Session_025


February 16, 1981

1981年2月16日

## (25.0)

(25.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and the light of the Infinite Creator. We communicate now.

(25.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (25.1)

(25.1) Questioner . . . asking first what cause or complex of causes has led to the instrument’s chest cold, as it is called?

(25.1) 发问者 ……首先想问，是什么原因或复合原因导致了器皿的所谓胸部感冒？

Ra I am Ra. This distortion towards illness was caused by the free will of the instrument in accepting a chemical substance which you call LSD. This was carefully planned by those entities which do not desire this instrument to remain viable. The substance has within it the facility of removing large stores of vital energy from the ingestor.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个朝向疾病的变貌是由于器皿的自由意志，接受了一种你们称为 LSD 的化学物质。这被那些不希望该器皿保持活力的实体精心策划。该物质具有从摄取者身上移除大量生命能量的能力。

The first hope of the Orion entity which arranged this opportunity was that this instrument would become less polarized towards what you call the positive. Due to conscious efforts upon the part of this instrument, using the substance as a programmer for service to others and for thankfulness, this instrument was spared this distortion and there was no result satisfactory to the Orion group.

安排这次机会的猎户座实体的第一个希望是，该器皿会变得不那么朝向你们所谓的正面极化。由于该器皿有意识地努力，将该物质用作服务他人和感恩的编程者，该器皿得以免于这一变貌，并且没有产生令猎户座集团满意的结果。

The second hope lay in the possible misuse of the most powerful means of transmission of energy between your peoples in the area of body-complex distortions. We have not previously spoken of the various types of energy blockages and transfers, positive and negative, that may take place due to participation in your sexual reproductive complex of actions. This entity, however, is a very strong entity with very little distortion from universal green-ray love energy. Thus this particular plan was not effected either, as the entity continued to give of itself in this context in an open, or green-ray, manner rather than attempting to receive,[38] or to manipulate, other-self.

第二个希望在于，在身体复合体变貌的领域中，可能滥用你们人群之间最强大的能量传输方式。我们之前没有谈论过，因参与你们的性生殖复合行为而可能发生的各种正面和负面能量阻塞与传输类型。然而，这个实体是一个非常强大的实体，来自宇宙绿色光芒之爱能量的变貌非常少。因此，这个特定的计划也未能得逞，因为该实体继续在这个情境中以开放的（或绿色光芒的）方式奉献自己，而不是试图从其他自我那里接收[38]或操控其他自我。

The only remaining distortion available, since this entity would not detune and would not cease sharing love universally under this chemical substance, was simply to drain this entity of as much energy as possible. This entity has a strong distortion towards busyness which it has been attempting to overcome for some time, realizing it not to be the appropriate attitude for this work. In this particular area the ingestion of this substance did indeed, shall we say, cause distortions away from viability due to the busyness and the lack of desire to rest; this instrument staying alert for much longer than appropriate. Thus much vital energy was lost, making this instrument unusually susceptible to infections such as it now experiences.

由于该实体不会失谐，也不会在这种化学物质下停止普世地分享爱，唯一剩下的可用的变貌就是尽可能多地消耗该实体的能量。该实体有一种朝向忙碌的强烈变貌，她已经试图克服一段时间了，意识到这对这项工作来说不是合适的态度。在这个特定领域中，摄入这种物质确实——容我们说——由于忙碌和缺乏休息的意愿而导致了远离生命力的变貌；该器皿保持清醒的时间远超适当范围。因此，大量生命能量流失了，使该器皿异常容易受到像现在这样的感染。

## (25.2)

(25.2) Questioner The second question the instrument requested was: "How may I best revitalize myself, not only now, but in the future?"

(25.2) 发问者 器皿请求的第二个问题是："我如何才能最好地恢复活力，不仅是现在，也包括将来？"

Ra I am Ra. This instrument is aware of the basic needs of its constitution, those being: meditation, acceptance of limitations, experiences of joy through association with others and with the beauty as of the singing; and the exercising with great contact, whenever possible, with the life forces of second density, especially those of trees; this entity also needing to be aware of the moderate but steady intake of foodstuffs, exercise being suggested at a fairly early portion of the day and at a later portion of the day before the resting.

Ra 我是 Ra。该器皿意识到其体质的根本需求，这些需求是：冥想、接受限制、通过与他人交往以及通过像歌唱之美来体验喜悦；以及在可能时，尽可能多地与第二密度的生命力量（特别是树木的生命力量）进行深度接触；该实体还需要意识到适度但稳定的食物摄入，建议在一天中较早的时段以及一天中较晚的、休息前的时段进行运动。

## (25.3)

(25.3) Questioner The third question that she requested to be asked was: "How may Don and Jim help to revitalize me?"

(25.3) 发问者 她请求提出的第三个问题是："Don 和 Jim 如何帮助我恢复活力？"

Ra I am Ra. This is not an appropriate question for full answer. We can say only that these entities are most conscientious. We may add that due to this instrument's distortion towards imbalance in the space/time nexus, it would be well were this entity accompanied during exercise.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个问题不适合给予完整回答。我们只能表示，这些实体是极其尽责的。我们可以补充，由于该器皿在空间/时间连结中朝向了不平衡的变貌，如果该实体在锻炼时有人陪伴将会更好。

## (25.4)

(25.4) Questioner Thank you. We shall now continue with the material from yesterday. You stated that about 3,000 years ago the Orion group left due to Diaspora. Was the Confederation then able to make any progress after the Orion group left?

(25.4) 发问者 谢谢你。我们现在将继续昨天的话题。你说过大约 3,000 年前，猎户座集团因大流散而离开。那么，星际联邦在猎户座集团离开后是否有取得任何进展？

Ra I am Ra. For many of your centuries, both the Confederation and the Orion Confederation busied themselves with each other upon planes above your own, shall we say, planes in time/space whereby machinations were conceived and the armor of light girded on. Battles have been and are continuing to be fought upon these levels.

Ra 我是 Ra。在你们的许多世纪中，星际联邦和猎户座联邦都在高于你们自身的层面上——容我们说，时间/空间中的层面上——彼此忙于较量，在那里构想了各种计谋，并穿上了光之盔甲。战斗在这些层面上已经发生，并且仍在继续。

Upon the earth plane, energies had been set in motion which did not cause a great deal of call. There were isolated instances of callings, one such taking place beginning approximately two six zero zero [2,600] of your years in the past in what you would call Greece at this time, and resulting in writings and understandings of some facets of the Law of One. We especially note the one known as Thales and the one known as Heraclitus, those being of the philosopher career, as you may call it, teaching their students. We also point out the understandings of the one known as Pericles.

在地球层面上，已经启动的能量并未引起大量呼求。存在一些孤立的呼求实例，其中一次始于大约你们过去 2,600 年前，在你们此时称为希腊的地方，并产生了关于一的法则某些面向的著作和理解。我们特别指出那位被称为泰勒斯（Thales）的人和那位被称为赫拉克利特（Heraclitus）的人，他们是——如你们可称之为——哲学家职业，教导他们的学生。我们也指出那位被称为伯里克利（Pericles）的人的理解。

At this time there was a limited amount of visionary information which the Confederation was allowed to telepathically impress. However, for the most part during this time empires died and rose according to the attitudes and energies set in motion long ago, not resulting in strong polarization but rather in that mixture of the positive and the warlike, or negative, which has been characteristic of this final minor cycle of your beingness.

在这段时间里，星际联邦被允许通过心电感应印入的洞见信息数量有限。然而，在这段时间的大部分时间里，帝国根据很久以前启动的态度和能量而兴衰，并未产生强烈的极化，而是产生了正面与好战（或负面）的混合，这一直是你们存在性的这个最后小周期的特征。

## (25.5)

(25.5) Questioner You spoke of an Orion Confederation and a battle being fought between the Confederation and the Orion Confederation. Is it possible to convey any concept of how this battle is fought?

(25.5) 发问者 你提到了猎户座联邦，以及星际联邦与猎户座联邦之间正在进行的战斗。是否有可能传达关于这场战斗如何进行的任何概念？

Ra I am Ra. Picture, if you will, your mind. Picture it then in total unity with all other minds of your society. You are then single-minded, and that which is a weak electrical charge in your physical illusion is now an enormously powerful machine whereby thoughts may be projected as things.

Ra 我是 Ra。如果你愿意，请想象你的心智。然后想象它与你们社会中所有其他心智完全合一。那时你们就是同心一意的，而且在你们的物理幻象中只是微弱电荷的东西，现在变成了一台极其强大的机器，通过它，思想可以作为事物被投射出去。

In this endeavor the Orion group charges, or attacks, the Confederation arms with light. The result, a standoff, as you would call it, both energies being somewhat depleted by this and needing to regroup; the negative depleted through failure to manipulate, the positive depleted through failure to accept that which is given.

在这一努力中，猎户座集团用光冲击或攻击星际联邦的武装。结果是——如你们会说的——一种僵局，双方的能量都因此有所消耗，并需要重组；负面一方因操控失败而消耗，正面一方因未能接受所给予之物而消耗。

## (25.6)

(25.6) Questioner Could you amplify the meaning of what you said by "failure to accept that which is given?"

(25.6) 发问者 你能详细说明你所说的"未能接受所给予之物"是什么意思吗？

Ra I am Ra. At the level of time/space at which this takes place in the form of what you may call thought-war, the most accepting and loving energy would be to so love those who wished to manipulate that those entities were surrounded, engulfed, and transformed by positive energies.

Ra 我是 Ra。在时间/空间的层面上，这以你们可称之为思想战争的形式发生，最具接纳性和爱心的能量就是如此深爱那些想要操控的实体，以至于这些实体被正面能量所包围、吞没和转化。

This, however, being a battle of equals, the Confederation is aware that it cannot, on equal footing, allow itself to be manipulated in order to remain purely positive, for then, though pure, it would not be of any consequence, having been placed by the so-called powers of darkness under the heel, as you may say.

然而，由于这是一场势均力敌的战斗，星际联邦意识到，它不能在平等的基础上允许自己被操控以求保持纯粹正面，因为那样的话，虽然纯粹，却不会有任何影响力，已然被那所谓的黑暗力量践踏在脚下，如你们可能会说的。

It is thus that those who deal with this thought-war must be defensive rather than accepting in order to preserve their usefulness in service to others. Thusly, they cannot accept fully what the Orion Confederation wishes to give, that being enslavement. Thusly, some polarity is lost due to this friction, and both sides, if you will, must then regroup.

因此，那些处理这场思想战争的实体必须采取防御而非接纳，以保持它们在服务他人中的有用性。因此，它们不能完全接受猎户座联邦想要给予的东西，即奴役。因此，由于这种摩擦，一些极性丧失了，而双方——如果你愿意——都必须重组。

It has not been fruitful for either side. The only consequence which has been helpful is a balancing of the energies available to this planet so that these energies have less necessity to be balanced in this space/time, thus lessening the chances of planetary annihilation.

这对双方都没有产生成果。唯一有帮助的结果是，可供这个行星使用的能量得到了平衡，使得这些能量在这个空间/时间中较少需要被平衡，从而减少了行星毁灭的可能性。

## (25.7)

(25.7) Questioner Very important point, I believe. Does a portion of the Confederation then engage in this thought battle? What percentage engages?

(25.7) 发问者 我相信这是非常重要的一点。那么，星际联邦的一部分是否参与了这场思想战斗？参与的比例是多少？

Ra I am Ra. This is the most difficult work of the Confederation. Only four planetary entities at any one time are asked to partake in this conflict.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是星际联邦最困难的工作。在任何时候，只有四个行星实体被要求参与这场冲突。

## (25.8)

(25.8) Questioner What density are these four planetary entities?

(25.8) 发问者 这四个行星实体是什么密度？

Ra I am Ra. These entities are of the density of love, numbering four.

Ra 我是 Ra。这些实体是爱的密度，共四个。

## (25.9)

(25.9) Questioner Then I am assuming this is the most effective density for this work. Would this density—an entity of this density—be more effective for this type of work than, say, an entity of density five or six?

(25.9) 发问者 那么我假设这是最适合这项工作的密度。这个密度的实体——该密度的一个实体——会比，比如说，第五或第六密度的实体更有效地从事这类工作吗？

Ra I am Ra. The fourth density is the only density besides your own which, lacking the wisdom to refrain from battle, sees the necessity of the battle. Thus it is necessary that fourth-density social memory complexes be used.

Ra 我是 Ra。第四密度是除了你们自身密度之外，唯一一个因缺乏抑制战斗的智慧而看到战斗必要性的密度。因此，必须使用第四密度社会记忆复合体。

## (25.10)

(25.10) Questioner Am I correct in assuming, then, that fourth density on both sides of the . . . of both the Orion and the Confederation sides are in this battle, and that the densities fifth and sixth on the Orion side do not engage in this? Is this correct?

(25.10) 发问者 那么，我假设双方——猎户座和星际联邦双方的第四密度——都参与了这场战斗，而猎户座一方的第五和第六密度则不参与其中，我的假设是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This will be the last full question as this entity's energies are low.

Ra 我是 Ra。这将是最后一个完整的问题，因为该实体的能量已经偏低。

It is partially correct. Fifth- and sixth-density entities positive would not take part in this battle. Fifth-density negative would not take part in this battle. Thus, the fourth density of both orientations join in this conflict.

这在一定程度上是正确的。第五和第六密度的正面实体不会参与这场战斗。第五密度的负面实体也不会参与这场战斗。因此，两种导向的第四密度都加入了这场冲突。

May we ask for a few short questions before we close?

在我们结束之前，我们可以请求几个简短的问题吗？

## (25.11)

(25.11) Questioner Well, I will first ask the answer at the end if there's anything we can do to make the instrument really comfortable. I'll only ask the questions that the instrument has—or answer the questions that the instrument has energy left to give, but I really would like to know of the orientation of fifth-density negative for not participating in this battle.

(25.11) 发问者 好的，我会在最后先问我们是否能做些什么来让器皿真正舒适。我只会在器皿有——或者回答器皿还有能量给出的问题，但我真的很想知道第五密度负面实体不参与这场战斗的导向原因。

Ra I am Ra. The fifth density is the density of light, or wisdom. The so-called negative service-to-self entity in this density is at an high level of awareness and wisdom and has ceased activity except by thought. The fifth-density negative is extraordinarily compacted and separated from all else.

Ra 我是 Ra。第五密度是光或智慧的密度。在这个密度中，所谓的负面服务自我实体处于高度的觉知和智慧水平，并且除了通过思想之外已停止活动。第五密度负面实体是极其致密化并与所有其他一切分离的。

## (25.12)

(25.12) Questioner Thank you very much. We do not wish to deplete the instrument, so is there anything that we can do to make the instrument more comfortable?

(25.12) 发问者 非常感谢你。我们不希望耗尽器皿的能量，所以有没有什么我们可以做的来让器皿更加舒适？

Ra I am Ra. You are very conscientious. As we requested previously, it would be well to observe the angles taken by the more upright posture of the entity. It is causing some nerve blockage in the portion of the body complex called the elbows.

Ra 我是 Ra。你们非常尽责。正如我们之前请求的，最好观察该实体更直立姿势所采取的角度。这正在身体复合体被称为肘部的部分引起一些神经阻塞。

I am Ra. I leave you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. Go forth, then, rejoicing in the power and the peace of the One Creator. Adonai.

我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中离开你们。那么，向前去吧，在太一造物主的大能与和平中欢庆。Adonai。
