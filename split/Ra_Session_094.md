# § Ra Session_094


August 26, 1982

1982年8月26日

## (94.0)

(94.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. I communicate now.

(94.0) Ra 我是Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我现在开始通讯。

## (94.1)

(94.1) Questioner Could you first please give me the condition of the instrument?

(94.1) 发问者 你能首先告诉我器皿的状态吗？

Ra I am Ra. There is some small increase in physical energy deficit. It is not substantial. All else is as at the previous asking.

Ra 我是Ra。身体能量亏缺有轻微增加。不是实质性的。其他一切都与上次询问时一样。

## (94.2)

(94.2) Questioner I have questions here from the instrument. One: “Is our fifth-density friend responsible for the instrument’s extreme distortion towards pain during and just after sessions?”

(94.2) 发问者 我这里有一些来自器皿的问题。第一：“我们的第五密度朋友是否对器皿在集会期间和刚结束后极度朝向疼痛的变貌负责？”

Ra I am Ra. Yes.

Ra 我是Ra。是的。

## (94.3)

(94.3) Questioner Is there anything that we can do that we are not doing to remedy this situation so that the instrument does not experience this pain or as much of it?

(94.3) 发问者 有什么我们没在做的事情可以补救这种情况，使器皿不会经历这种疼痛或经历更少的疼痛？

Ra I am Ra. There is little that can be done due to a complex of pre-existing distortions. The distortions are triple in the source:

Ra 我是Ra。由于预先存在的变貌复合体，几乎没有什么可以做的。这些变貌在来源上是三重的：

There is the, shall we say, less-than-adequate work of your chirurgeons which allows for various distortions in the left wrist area.[102]

有——容我们说——你们外科医生不够充分的工作，导致了左腕区域的各种变貌。[102]

There is the distortion called systemic lupus erythematosus which causes the musculature of the lower left and right arms to allow for distortions in the normal, shall we say, configuration of both.

有被称为系统性红斑狼疮的变貌，它导致左下臂和右下臂的肌肉组织允许——容我们说——两者正常配置中的变貌。

Lastly, there is the nerve damage, more especially to the left, but in both appendages from the thoracic outlet.

最后，有神经损伤，尤其是在左侧，但双侧附肢从胸廓出口处都有。

In the course of the waking behavior the instrument can respond to the various signals which ring the tocsin of pain, thus alerting the mind complex, which in turn moves the physical complex in many and subtle configurations which relieve the various distortions. Your friend greets these distortions, as has been stated before, immediately prior to the beginning of the working.[103]

在清醒行为的过程中，器皿能够响应各种敲响疼痛警钟的信号，从而警示心智复合体，心智复合体又反过来以许多微妙的方式移动身体复合体，从而缓解各种变貌。正如先前所述，你们的朋友在集会开始之前立即问候这些变貌。[103]

However, during the working the instrument is not with its yellow-ray chemical vehicle, and thusly the many small movements which could most effectively aid in the decrease of these distortions is not possible. Ra must carefully examine the mental configurations of the mind complex in order to make even the grossest manipulation. It is not our skill to use a yellow-ray vehicle.

然而，在集会期间，器皿不与其黄光化学载具同在，因此，那些最能有效帮助减少这些变貌的许多小动作是不可能的。Ra必须仔细检查心智复合体的心智配置，才能做出哪怕是最粗略的操作。使用黄光载具不是我们的技能。

The weight of the cover has some deleterious effect upon these distortions in some cases, and thus we mentioned that there was a small thing which could be done; that is, the framing of that which lifted the coverlet from the body slightly. In order to compensate for loss of warmth the wearing of material warming the manual appendages would then be indicated.

在某些情况下，覆盖物的重量对这些变貌有一些有害影响，因此我们提到有一件小事可以做；那就是用一个框架将床罩从身体上稍微抬起。为了补偿热量的损失，穿戴保暖手部附肢的材料将是合适的。

## (94.7)

(94.7) Questioner Could you tell me why I have felt so extremely tired on several recent occasions?

(94.7) 发问者 你能告诉我为什么我最近几次感到如此极度疲倦吗？

Ra I am Ra. This has been covered in previous material.[105]

Ra 我是Ra。这在先前的材料中已涵盖。[105]

The contact which you now experience costs a certain amount of the energy which each of the group brought into manifestation in the present incarnation. Although the brunt of this cost falls upon the instrument, it is caparisoned by pre-incarnative design with the light and gladsome armor of faith and will to a far more conscious extent than most mind/body/spirit complexes are able to enjoy without much training and initiation.[106]

你们现在所经历的通讯消耗了小组中每个人在此次化身中带入显化的一定量的能量。尽管这个代价的主要部分落在器皿身上，但通过化身前的设计，它以信仰和意志的光明而喜悦的铠甲装备，其程度远超过大多数心/身/灵复合体在没有大量训练和启蒙的情况下所能享有的。[106]

Those of the support group also offer the essence of will and faith in service to others, supporting the instrument as it releases itself completely in the service of the One Creator. Therefore, each of the support group also experiences a weariness of the spirit which is indistinguishable from physical energy deficit, except that if each experiments with this weariness, each shall discover the physical energy in its usual distortion.

支持小组的成员也以服务他人的意志和信仰之本质，支持着器皿，因为器皿将自己完全释放于太一造物主的服务中。因此，支持小组的每个成员也经历着灵性的疲倦，这与物理能量亏缺难以区分，除非每个人对这种疲倦进行实验，每个人将发现物理能量处于其通常的变貌中。

## (94.8)

(94.8) 发问者 谢谢你。我真的不是故意要重复先前的材料。我应该更仔细地措辞我的问题，这样……那就是我所期望的。我只是想确认我所怀疑的事实。从现在起我会更仔细地提问。

It seems to me that I coast on the spiritual gifts given at birth and never have spent any time getting to know my human self which seems to be a child, immature and irrational. Is this so?”

来自器皿的问题：“在度假期间，我发现了许多关于自己以前没有意识到的方面。在我看来，我靠着出生时被给予的灵性天赋顺流而行，从未花时间去了解我的人类自我，它似乎是一个孩子，不成熟且不理性。是这样吗？”

Ra I am Ra. This is partially correct.

Ra 我是Ra。这在一定程度上是正确的。

## (94.9)

(94.9) Questioner Then she says: “If this is so, this seems to be part of the riddle about the manner of beingness that Ra spoke of. I fear if I do not work successfully on my human distortions I shall be responsible for losing the contact. Yet also Ra suggests the over-dedication to any outcome is unwise. Could Ra comment on these thoughts?”

(94.9) 发问者 然后她说：“如果是这样，这似乎是Ra所说的关于存在方式的谜题的一部分。我害怕如果我不能成功地处理我的人类变貌，我将为失去这个通讯负责。然而Ra也建议对任何结果的过度执着是不明智的。Ra能对这些想法发表评论吗？”

Ra I am Ra. We comment in general, first upon the query about the contact which indicates, once again, that the instrument views the mind/body/spirit complex with jaundiced eye.

Ra 我是Ra。我们一般性地评论，首先关于通讯的问题，这再次表明器皿以偏见之眼看待心/身/灵复合体。

Each mind/body/spirit complex that is seeking shall almost certainly have the immature and irrational behaviors. It is also the case that this entity—as well as almost all seekers—has done substantial work within the framework of the incarnative experience and has, indeed, developed maturity and rationality. That this instrument should fail to see that which has been accomplished and see only that which remains to be accomplished may well be noted. Indeed, any seeker discovering in itself this complex of mental and mental/emotional distortions shall ponder the possible non-efficacy of judgment.

每个寻求的心/身/灵复合体几乎肯定会有不成熟和不理性的行为。同样，这个实体——以及几乎所有寻求者——在化身经验的框架内已经完成了实质性的工作，并且确实发展了成熟和理性。这个器皿未能看到已经完成的事，而只看到尚待完成的事，这很值得注意。确实，任何寻求者若在自己身上发现这种心智和心智/情感变貌的复合体，应当思考判断可能无效。

As we approach the second portion of the query we view the possibility of infringement upon free will. However, we believe we may make reply within the boundaries of the Law of Confusion.

当我们接近问题的第二部分时，我们看到了侵犯自由意志的可能性。然而，我们相信我们可以在混淆法则的范围内做出回答。

This particular instrument was not trained, nor did it study, nor worked it at any discipline in order to contact Ra. We were able, as we have said many times, to contact this group using this instrument because of the purity of this instrument’s dedication to the service of the One Infinite Creator, and also because of the great amount of harmony and acceptance enjoyed each by each within the group; this situation making it possible for the support group to function without significant distortion.

这个特定的器皿没有受过训练，没有学习，也没有在任何学科中努力以接触Ra。正如我们多次说过的，我们能够使用这个器皿接触这个小组，是因为这个器皿对服务太一无限造物主的奉献之纯净，也是因为小组中每个人彼此之间享有的巨大和谐与接纳；这种情况使支持小组能够没有显著变貌地运作。

We are humble messengers. How can any thought be taken by an instrument as to the will of the Creator?

我们是谦卑的信使。一个器皿怎能对造物主的意志有任何想法呢？

We thank this group that we may speak through it, but the future is mazed. We cannot know whether our geste may, after one final working, be complete.[107]

我们感谢这个小组，我们可以通过它说话，但未来是迷宫般的。我们无法知道我们的功业是否会在最后一次工作后完成。[107]

Can the instrument, then, think for a moment that it shall cease in the service of the One Infinite Creator? We ask the instrument to ponder these queries and observations.

那么，器皿能想一下它将在服务太一无限造物主中停止吗？我们请器皿思考这些问题和观察。

(94.10) 发问者 在上一次集会中，有一个陈述说，对最看似清晰的观察，很多东西都被遮蔽了。Ra能扩展一下那个陈述的意思吗？我假设这意味着所有超出我们所谓物理感知范围的东西——与光谱等有关——都被遮蔽了，但我也直觉到有更多东西被遮蔽了。Ra能扩展那个概念吗？

Ra 我是Ra。你的假设是敏锐的。确实，我们的意思并非暗示你们当前幻象的物理装置作为面纱过程的一部分是有限的。你们的物理限制本就如此。

However, because of the unique biases of each mind/body/spirit complex, there are sometimes quite simple instances of distortion when there is no apparent cause for such distortion. Let us use the example of the virile and immature male who meets and speaks clearly with a young female whose physical form has the appropriate configuration to cause, for this male entity, the activation of the red-ray sexual arousal.

然而，由于每个心/身/灵复合体的独特偏见，有时会出现相当简单的变貌实例，却没有明显的变貌原因。让我们以一个有活力但不成熟的男性为例，他遇到一位年轻女性并清晰地交谈，该女性的身体形态具有适当的配置，对这个男性实体而言，导致了红光性唤起的激活。

The words spoken may be upon a simple subject such as naming, information as to the occupation, and various other common interchanges of sound vibratory complex. The male entity, however, is using almost all the available consciousness it possesses in registering the desirability of the female. Such may also be true of the female.

所说的话语可能涉及一个简单的主题，如姓名、职业信息以及其他各种常见的声音振动复合体交流。然而，男性实体几乎使用了它拥有的所有可用意识来记录该女性的可欲性。女性也可能同样如此。

Thusly an entire exchange of information may be meaningless because the actual catalyst is of the body. This is unconsciously controlled and is not a conscious decision. This example is simplistic.

因此，整个信息交流可能毫无意义，因为实际的催化剂是身体的。这是无意识地控制的，并非一个有意识的决定。这个例子是简化的。
