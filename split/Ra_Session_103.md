# § Ra Session_103


June 10, 1983
1983年6月10日

(103.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

(103.1) Questioner Could you first please give me the condition of the instrument?
(103.1) 发问者 你能否先告诉我这个器皿的状态？

Ra 我是 Ra。器皿的物理变貌仍然严重。

Further, the vital energies of this mind/body/spirit complex are much diminished, although acceptable for the needs of this working. This is to be noted as the lowest, or most distorted, vital reading of this all-important energy.
此外，这个心/身/灵复合体的生命能量大幅降低，尽管对这次工作的需要来说可以接受。这应被注意到是这种至关重要能量的最低、或最变貌的生命能量读数。

The mental and mental/emotional distortions are as last seen.
心智和心智/情感变貌与上次所见相同。

We find the will of the instrument, having been unwisely used, to have encouraged the distortions of vital energy. It is well that the instrument ponder this.
我们发现器皿的意志被不明智地使用，助长了生命能量的变貌。器皿思考这一点是好的。

(103.3) Ra 我是 Ra。管道现在令人满意。我们发现器皿的黄色光芒化学身体已筋疲力尽，但正在尝试通过运动和饮食等行动来改善。

我们可以说，感染还没有完全离开身体复合体，尽管它已经远没有那么刺激性了。

## (103.4)

(103.4) Questioner The instrument asks the question why she lost her joy in the recent past? Would Ra comment, please?
(103.4) 发问者 器皿问为什么她在最近失去了她的喜悦？Ra 能否请评论一下？

Ra I am Ra. The instrument made a free-will decision not to address the physical catalyst causing great pain by means of the allopathically prescribed chemical compound, which the instrument was sure would be efficacious due to its reliance upon the suggestions of Ra.
Ra 我是 Ra。器皿做出了一个自由意志的决定，不用对抗疗法开处的化学复合物来处理引起巨大疼痛的物理催化剂，器皿由于依赖 Ra 的建议而确信这种复合物会有效。

Thus the catalyst was given in a more complete form. The outer service to others became nearly impossible, causing the entity to experience, once again, the choice of the martyr; that is, to put value in a fatal action and die, or to put value on consciousness of the creation and of the One Creator and, thereby, live. The instrument, through will, chose the latter path.
因此，催化剂以更完整的形式被给予。外在的服务他人变得几乎不可能，导致该实体再次经历了殉道者的选择；即，将价值放在致命的行动上而死去，或将价值放在对造物和太一造物主的意识上而活下去。器皿通过意志选择了后者的道路。

However, the mind and mental/emotional distortions did not give the support to this decision necessary to maintain a state of unity which this entity normally experiences and has experienced since its incarnation’s beginnings.
然而，心智和心智/情感变貌没有给这个决定提供维持该实体通常体验到并且自其投生之初以来一直体验到的合一状态所必需的支持。

Since this catalyst has been accepted, the work begun to remove distortions blocking the indigo ray might well be continued apace.
由于这个催化剂已被接受，开始清除阻塞靛蓝光芒的变貌的工作可以继续迅速进行。

## (103.5)

(103.5) Questioner Could Ra recommend work appropriate for removing indigo-ray blockage?
(103.5) 发问者 Ra 能否推荐适合清除靛蓝光芒阻塞的工作？

Ra I am Ra. We cannot recommend for the general situation, for in each case the distortional vortex is unique. In this particular nexus, the more appropriate working is in the mental and mental/emotional powers of analysis and observation. When the strongest and least distorted complex is set in support, then the less strong portions of the complex shall be strengthened.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们无法为一般情况推荐，因为在每种情况下变貌漩涡都是独特的。在这个特定的关联点上，更合适的工作在于分析和观察的心智和心智/情感力量。当最强大且变貌最小的复合体被设定为支持时，那么复合体中较不强大的部分就会被增强。

This entity has long worked with this catalyst. However, this is the first occasion wherein the drugs to dull the pain that sharpens the catalyst have been refused.
这个实体长期以来一直在与这个催化剂一同工作。然而，这是第一次拒绝使用麻痹疼痛以加剧催化剂的药物。

Ra 我是 Ra。这个实体在你们的一段空间/时间中一直处于休息状态。然而，它已被警告正在发生的工作，并且很快将再次成为你们的同伴。

(103.8) Questioner I didn’t mean to cover previously covered material. I meant to add any of this to specifically focus on at this time, the best possible thing that we or the instrument could do to improve these energies, the salient activity.
(103.8) 发问者 我不是想要涵盖先前已经涵盖的材料。我的意思是添加任何在此时特别聚焦的内容，即我们或器皿可以做的最好的事情来改善这些能量，突出的活动。

Ra I am Ra. Before responding we ask your vigilance during pain flares as the channel is acceptable but is being distorted periodically by the severe physical distortions of the yellow-ray, chemical body of the instrument.
Ra 我是 Ra。在回答之前，我们请你们在疼痛发作期间保持警觉，因为管道是可接受的，但正在被器皿的黄色光芒化学身体的严重物理变貌周期性地扭曲。

Those salient items for the support group are praise and thanksgiving in harmony. These the group has accomplished with such a degree of acceptability that we cavil not at the harmony of the group.[133]
对支持小组来说，那些突出的项目是和谐中的赞美和感恩。这些小组已经以如此程度的可接受性完成了，以至于我们对小组的和谐毫无挑剔。[133]

As to the instrument, the journey from worth-in-action to worth-in-esse is arduous.[134] The entity has denied itself in order to be free from that which it calls addiction.[135] This sort of martyrdom—and here we speak of the small but symbolically great sacrifice of the clothing—causes the entity to frame a selfhood in poorness which feeds unworthiness, unless the poverty is seen to be true richness.
至于器皿，从行动之价值到存在之价值的旅程是艰辛的。[134] 该实体否定了自己，以便摆脱它所称为上瘾的东西。[135] 这种殉道——在这里我们谈到的是衣服的微小但象征性地伟大的牺牲——导致该实体用贫乏来塑造自我身份，这滋养了不配得感，除非贫乏被看作真正的富足。

In other words, good works for the wrong reasons cause confusion and distortion. We encourage the instrument to value itself and to see that its true requirements are valued by the self. We suggest contemplation of true richness of being.
换句话说，出于错误原因的善行会导致混乱和变貌。我们鼓励器皿珍视自己，并看到其真正的需求被自我所珍视。我们建议对存在的真正富足进行冥想。

## (103.11)

(103.11) Questioner Would Ra comment on that?
(103.11) 发问者 Ra 能否评论这一点？

Ra I am Ra. The use of the tau and the architect’s square is, indeed, intended to suggest the proximity of the space/time of the Great Way’s environment to time/space.[136] We find this observation most perceptive.
Ra 我是 Ra。T 字形和建筑师方尺的使用确实是为了暗示大道环境的空间/时间与时间/空间的接近性。[136] 我们发现这个观察非常敏锐。

我们可以说，大道的整个氛围确实取决于它与意义者的显著差异。意义者是有意义的自我，在很大程度上但不完全地受到帘幕降低的影响。

The Great Way of the Mind, the Body, or the Spirit draws the environment which has been the new architecture caused by the veiling process and, thusly, dipped in the great, limitless current of time/space.
心智、身体或灵性的大道牵引着那个环境——该环境是由帘幕过程产生的新架构，因此被浸入时间/空间的伟大无限流动之中。

## (103.12)

(103.12) Questioner I am guessing that the wheels on this chariot indicate the ability of the mind to be able now to move in time/space. Is this correct?
(103.12) 发问者 我猜测这辆战车上的轮子表示心智现在能够在时间/空间中移动的能力。这正确吗？

Ra I am Ra. We cannot say that the observation is totally incorrect, for there is as much work in time/space as the individual who evokes this complex of concepts has assimilated.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们不能说这个观察完全错误，因为在时间/空间中有多少工作，就像引发这个概念复合体的个体所吸收的那样多。

However, it would be more appropriate to draw the attention to the fact that although the chariot is wheeled, it is not harnessed to that which draws it by a physical or visible harness. What then, O student, links and harnesses the chariot’s power of movement to the chariot?
然而，更合适的做法是将注意力引向这个事实：尽管战车有轮子，它并没有通过物理或可见的缰绳与拉动它的东西相连接。那么，哦，学生，是什么将战车的移动力量与战车连接和缰绑起来？

## (103.14)

(103.14) Questioner The bent left leg of the two sphinxes indicates a transformation that occurs on the left that doesn’t on the right, possibly an inability in that position to move. Does this have any merit?
(103.14) 发问者 两个狮身人面像弯曲的左腿表示一种发生在左边而不发生在右边的转化，可能表示在那个位置无法移动。这有任何价值吗？

Ra I am Ra. The observation has merit in that it may serve as the obverse of the connotation intended.
Ra 我是 Ra。这个观察有价值，因为它可以作为所设想内涵的反面。

The position is intended to show two items, one of which is the dual possibilities of the time-full characters there drawn. The resting is possible in time, as is the progress. If a mixture is attempted, the upright, moving leg will be greatly hampered by the leg that is bent.
这个姿势旨在显示两个项目，其中之一是那里所描绘的充满时间的角色的双重可能性。在时间中可以休息，也可以前进。如果尝试混合，笔直的、移动的腿将会被弯曲的腿严重妨碍。

The other meaning has to do with the same right angle, with its architectural squareness, as the device upon the breast of the actor.
另一个含义与同样的直角有关，具有其建筑学上的方正性，如同演员胸口上的装置。

Time/space is close in this concept complex, brought close due to the veiling process and its efficaciousness in producing actors who wish to use the resources of the mind in order to evolve.
时间/空间在这个概念复合体中很接近，由于帘幕过程及其在产生希望使用心智资源以进化的演员方面的有效性而被拉近。
