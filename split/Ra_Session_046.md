# § Ra Session_046


April 15, 1981

1981年4月15日

## (46.0)

(46.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.

(46.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (46.1)

(46.1) Questioner Could we please first have an indication of the instrument's condition?

(46.1) 发问者 我们能否首先了解一下器皿的状态？

Ra I am Ra. This instrument scans at present at extremely low physical energy and is under attack. This will continue for some of your weeks. However, the vital energy of this instrument is gradually restoring its former level.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个器皿目前的扫描显示其身体能量极低，且正在遭受攻击。这种状况将持续你们数周之久。然而，这个器皿的生命能量正在逐步恢复到其先前的水平。

## (46.2)

(46.2) Questioner In that case, we'd better ask you only one question unless you deem it all right to ask any more questions than that. But the one question that is bothering us, which I got in meditation, may be an inappropriate question, but I feel it my duty to ask it because it is central to the instrument's mental condition and ours.

(46.2) 发问者 既然如此，我们最好只问你一个问题，除非你认为再问更多问题也无妨。但那个困扰我们的问题——我在冥想中得到的——可能是个不恰当的问题，但我感到有责任问出来，因为它对器皿和我们的心理状态至关重要。

It has to do with the two cats which we were going to take to have worked upon today for teeth cleaning and the small growth removed from Gandalf's leg. I got the impression that there might be an inroad there for the Orion group, and I was primarily concerned about anything that we could do for protection for these two cats. I may be out of line in asking this, but I feel it my duty to ask it. Would you please give me information, all the information you can on that subject?

这和我们今天打算带去的两只猫有关——它们需要清洁牙齿，还要去除甘道夫腿上的一个小增生物。我得到的印象是，猎户座集团可能借此机会侵入，我主要关心的是我们能做些什么来保护这两只猫。我可能不该问这个问题，但我感到有责任问出来。请你给我关于这个主题的所有信息，可以吗？

Ra I am Ra. The entity, mind/body/spirit complex, Gandalf, being harvestable third density, is open to the same type of psychic attack to which you yourselves are vulnerable. Therefore, through the mechanism of images and dreams, it is potentially possible for negative concepts to be offered to this mind/body/spirit complex, thus having possible deleterious results. The entity, Fairchild, though harvestable through investment, does not have the vulnerability to attack in as great an amount due to a lack of the mind complex activity in the distortion of conscious devotion.

Ra 我是 Ra。甘道夫这个实体，心/身/灵复合体，作为可收割的第三密度实体，容易受到与你们自身同样类型的通灵攻击。因此，通过影像和梦境的机制，负面概念有可能被提供给这个心/身/灵复合体，从而可能产生有害的结果。费尔柴尔德这个实体，尽管通过赋予而可收割，但由于缺乏有意识奉献这个变貌方面的心智复合体活动，其受攻击的脆弱性没有那么强。

For protection of these entities we might indicate two possibilities. Firstly, the meditation putting on the armor of light. Secondly, the repetition of short ritual sentences known to this instrument from the establishment which distorts spiritual oneness for this instrument. This instrument's knowledge will suffice. This will aid due to the alerting of many discarnate entities also aware of these ritual sentences. The meditation is appropriate at the time of the activity on behalf of these entities. The ritual may be repeated with efficacy from this time until the safe return at convenient intervals.

为了保护这些实体，我们可以指出两种可能性。第一，冥想着披上光之铠甲。第二，重复这个器皿所知的简短仪式语句，这些语句来自那个为这个器皿彰显灵性合一之变貌的机构。这个器皿的知识就足够了。这将有所帮助，因为许多同样知晓这些仪式语句的无形实体会因此警觉。在为这些实体进行活动时进行冥想是合适的。仪式则可以从现在开始到安全返回为止，以方便的间隔有效重复。

Ra 我是 Ra。后者是正确的。你们可以继续。我们将监测这个器皿的生命能量。

## (46.7)

(46.7) Questioner I was just wondering, if an entity polarizes toward the service-to-self path, would the anger have the same physical effect that it affects an entity polarized toward the service-to-others path? Would it also cause cancer, or is it just a catalytic effect working in the positively polarized entity?

(46.7) 发问者 我只是在想，如果一个实体朝向服务自我之路极化，愤怒是否会对它产生与对朝向服务他人之路极化的实体相同的身体影响？它是否也会导致癌症，还是说这只是一种在正面极化实体中起作用的催化剂效应？

Ra I am Ra. The catalytic mechanisms are dependent not upon the chosen polarity of a mind/body/spirit complex, but upon the use, or purpose, to which this catalysis is put. Thus the entity which uses the experience of anger to polarize consciously, positively or negatively, does not experience the bodily catalyst but rather uses the catalyst in mental configuration.

Ra 我是 Ra。催化剂机制不取决于一个心/身/灵复合体所选择的极性，而是取决于这个催化作用被用于何种用途或目的。因此，一个利用愤怒体验来有意识地极化——无论是正面还是负面——的实体，不会体验身体催化剂，而是在心智配置中使用催化剂。

## (46.8)

(46.8) Questioner Not sure that I understand that. Let's take some examples. If an entity polarizing toward the negative path becomes angry . . . let's take the condition where he develops a cancer. What is the principle of that for him?

(46.8) 发问者 我不确定我理解了。让我们举一些例子。如果一个朝向负面之路极化的实体变得愤怒……让我们假设他患上癌症的情况。这对他而言的原理是什么？

Ra I am Ra. We see the thrust of your query and will respond at variance with the specific query if that meets with your approval.

Ra 我是 Ra。我们看到了你询问的意图，如果你同意的话，我们将以与具体问题有所不同的方式来回答。

## (46.9)

(46.9) Questioner Certainly.

(46.9) 发问者 当然可以。

Ra The entity polarizing positively perceives the anger. This entity, if using this catalyst mentally, blesses and loves this anger in itself. It then intensifies this anger consciously in mind alone until the folly of this red-ray energy is perceived, not as folly in itself, but as energy subject to spiritual entropy due to the randomness of energy being used.

Ra 正面极化的实体感知到愤怒。这个实体，如果在心智上使用这个催化剂，会祝福并爱这愤怒本身。然后，它会在心智中有意识地强化这愤怒，直到这红色光芒能量的愚蠢被感知到——不是作为愚蠢本身，而是作为由于能量被随机使用而遭受灵性熵增的能量。

Positive orientation then provides the will and faith to continue this mentally intense experience of letting the anger be understood, accepted, and integrated with the mind/body/spirit complex. The other-self which is the object of anger is thus transformed into an object of acceptance, understanding, and accommodation, all being reintegrated using the great energy which anger began.

正面导向随后提供了意志与信心，以继续这种心智上的强烈体验：让愤怒被理解、接纳，并与心/身/灵复合体整合。愤怒所指向的他者自我由此被转化为接纳、理解和包容的对象，一切都被重新整合，利用愤怒所启动的伟大能量。

The negatively oriented mind/body/spirit complex will use this anger in a similarly conscious fashion, refusing to accept the undirected, or random, energy of anger and instead, through will and faith, funneling this energy into a practical means of venting the negative aspect of this emotion so as to obtain control over other-self or otherwise control the situation causing anger.

负面导向的心/身/灵复合体会以同样有意识的方式使用这愤怒，拒绝接受愤怒中无定向的、或随机的能量，而是通过意志和信心，将这能量导入一个实际的手段，以宣泄这情绪的负面面向，从而获得对他者自我的控制，或以其他方式控制引发愤怒的情境。

Control is the key to negatively polarized use of catalyst. Acceptance is the key to positively polarized use of catalyst. Between these polarities lies the potential for this random and undirected energy creating a bodily complex analog of what you call the cancerous growth of tissue.

控制是负面极化使用催化剂的关键。接纳是正面极化使用催化剂的关键。在这两极之间，存在着这种随机且无定向的能量创造出一个肉体复合体类似物的可能性，也就是你们所说的癌性组织增生。

## (46.10)

(46.10) Questioner Then as I understand it you are saying that if the positively polarizing entity fails to accept the other-self or if the negatively polarizing entity fails to control the other-self, either of these conditions will cause cancer, possibly. Is this correct?

(46.10) 发问者 那么根据我的理解，你是说如果正面极化的实体未能接纳他者自我，或者如果负面极化的实体未能控制他者自我，这两种情况都可能导致癌症。这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This is partially correct. The first acceptance or control, depending upon polarity, is of the self. Anger is one of many things to be accepted and loved as a part of self or controlled as a part of self, if the entity is to do work.

Ra 我是 Ra。这在一定程度上是正确的。首先——取决于极性——是接纳或控制自我。愤怒是许多需要被接纳和爱的事物之一，作为自我的一部分；或者被控制，作为自我的一部分，如果该实体要进行工作的话。

## (46.11)

(46.11) Questioner Then are you saying that if a negatively polarized or polarizing entity is unable to control his own anger, or unable to control himself in anger, that he may cause cancer? Is this correct?

(46.11) 发问者 那么你是说，如果一个负面极化或正在极化的实体无法控制自己的愤怒，或无法在愤怒中控制自己，他就可能导致癌症？这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This is quite correct. The negative polarization contains a great requirement for control and repression.

Ra 我是 Ra。这完全正确。负面极化包含了对控制和压抑的巨大要求。

## (46.12)

(46.12) Questioner A repression of what?

(46.12) 发问者 压抑什么？

Ra I am Ra. Any mind-complex distortion which you may call emotional, which is of itself disorganized, needs, in order to be useful to the negatively oriented entity, to be repressed and then brought to the surface in an organized use. Thus you may find, for instance, negatively polarized entities controlling and repressing such basic bodily complex needs as the sexual desire in order that in the practice thereof the will may be used to enforce itself upon the other-self with greater efficiency when the sexual behavior is allowed.

Ra 我是 Ra。任何你们可以称之为情绪的心智复合体变貌，其本身是混乱无序的，为了对负面导向的实体有用，需要被压抑，然后以有组织的方式被带到表面来使用。因此你会发现，例如，负面极化的实体控制和压抑诸如性欲这类基本的肉体复合体需求，以便在允许性行为时，意志可以更有效地被用来强加于他者自我之上。

## (46.16)

(46.16) Questioner What is the plan for use of the catalyst of cancer?

(46.16) 发问者 癌症这个催化剂的使用计划是什么？

Ra I am Ra. The catalyst, and all catalyst, is designed to offer experience. This experience in your density may be loved and accepted, or it may be controlled. These are the two paths. When neither path is chosen, the catalyst fails in its design, and the entity proceeds until catalyst strikes it which causes it to form a bias towards acceptance and love or separation and control. There is no lack of space/time in which this catalyst may work.

Ra 我是 Ra。催化剂——以及所有催化剂——被设计来提供体验。这个体验在你们密度中可以被爱和接纳，或者可以被控制。这就是两条道路。当两条道路都没有被选择时，催化剂就未能达成其设计目的，实体继续前进，直到催化剂击中它，使其形成一种朝向接纳与爱，或朝向分离与控制的偏见。这个催化剂可以在其中发挥作用的空间/时间并不缺乏。
