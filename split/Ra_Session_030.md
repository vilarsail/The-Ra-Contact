# § Ra Session_030

## (30.0)

(30.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.

## (30.1)

(30.1) Questioner I am going to make a statement and then let you correct it if I have made any errors. This is the statement: creation is a single entity, or unity. If only a single entity exists, then the only concept of service is service to self. If this single entity subdivides, then the concept of service of one of its parts to the other part is born. From this concept springs the equality of service to self, or to others.

(30.1) 发问者 我将做一个陈述，如果有任何错误，请你纠正。以下是陈述：造物是单一实体，或合一。如果只有一个单一实体存在，那么服务的唯一概念就是服务自我。如果这个单一实体细分，那么其一部分服务另一部分的概念就诞生了。从这个概念中产生了服务自我与服务他人的平等性。

It would seem that as the Logos subdivided, parts would select each orientation. As individualized entities emerge in space/time then I would assume that they have polarity. Is this statement correct?

随着理则的细分，各部分似乎会选择各自的朝向。当个体化实体出现在空间/时间中时，我推测它们具有极性。这个陈述正确吗？

Ra I am Ra. This statement is quite perceptive and correct until the final phrase in which we note that the polarities begin to be explored only at the point when a third-density entity becomes aware of the possibility of choice between the concept, or distortion, of service to self or service to others. This marks the end of what you may call the unselfconscious, or innocent, phase of conscious awareness.

Ra 我是 Ra。这个陈述颇具洞察力且正确，直到最后一个分句；我们需要指出，极性仅在第三密度实体开始意识到可以在服务自我或服务他人的概念/变貌之间做出选择时才开始被探索。这标志着你们可称之为无自我意识或天真阶段的意识觉知的结束。

## (30.2)

(30.2) Questioner Thank you. Would you define mind, body, and spirit separately?

(30.2) 发问者 谢谢。你能否分别定义心智、身体和灵性？

Ra I am Ra. These terms are all simplistic descriptive terms which equal a complex of energy focuses; the body, as you call it, being the material of the density which you experience at a given space/time or time/space; this complex of materials being available for distortions of what you would call physical manifestation.

Ra 我是 Ra。这些术语都是简化的描述性术语，等同于一个能量聚焦的复合体；你们所称的身体，是你在特定空间/时间或时间/空间中体验的该密度之物质；这个物质复合体可供你们称之为物理显化的变貌所使用。

The mind is a complex which reflects the inpourings of the spirit and the up-pourings of the body complex. It contains what you know as feelings, emotions, and intellectual thoughts in its more conscious complexities. Moving further down the tree of mind we see the intuition, which is of the nature of the mind more in contact, or in tune, with the total beingness complex. Moving down to the roots of mind we find the progression of consciousness which gradually turns from the personal to the racial memory to the cosmic influxes, and thus becomes a direct contactor of that shuttle which we call the spirit complex.

心智是一个复合体，反映着灵性的注入和身体复合体的上涌。在其较有意识的层面中，它包含你们所知的感受、情感和智性思维。沿心智之树向下，我们看到直觉，它本质上是心智与整体存在复合体更紧密接触或调谐的部分。向下到达心智的根部，我们发现意识逐渐从个人记忆转向种族记忆，再转向宇宙注入，从而成为我们称之为灵性复合体之穿梭载具的直接接触者。

This spirit complex is the channel whereby the inpourings from all of the various universal, planetary, and personal inpourings may be funneled into the roots of consciousness, and whereby consciousness may be funneled to the gateway of intelligent infinity through the balanced intelligent energy of body and mind.[47]

这个灵性复合体是管道，通过它，来自所有各种宇宙、行星和个人层面的注入可以被汇集到意识的根部，同时意识也可以通过身体与心智的平衡智能能量被汇集到智能无限的入口。[47]

You will see by this series of definitive statements that mind, body, and spirit are inextricably intertwined and cannot continue, one without the other. Thus we refer to the mind/body/spirit complex rather than attempting to deal with them separately, for the work, shall we say, that you do during your experiences is done through the interaction of these three components, not through any one.

通过这一系列定义性陈述，你们将看到，心智、身体和灵性是密不可分地交织在一起的，缺一不可。因此，我们称之为心/身/灵复合体，而不是试图分别处理它们，因为——容我们说——你们在体验中所做的工作是通过这三个组成部分的互动完成的，而非通过任何单一部分。

Ra I am Ra. This is correct. The mind/body/spirit complex is quite intact; the physical body complex you now associate with the term "body" being but manifestation of a more dense, and intelligently informed, and powerful body complex.

Ra 我是 Ra。这是正确的。心/身/灵复合体相当完好；你们现在与"身体"一词关联的物理身体复合体，只是一个更致密、更智能且更强大的身体复合体的显化。

## (30.4)

(30.4) Questioner Is there any loss to the mind or spirit after this transition which we call death or any impairment of either because of the loss of this chemical body that we now have?

(30.4) 发问者 在我们称之为死亡的这一过渡之后，心智或灵性是否有任何损失，或由于失去我们现在拥有的这个化学身体而导致其中任何一个受到损害？

Ra I am Ra. In your terms, there is a great loss of mind complex due to the fact that much of the activity of a mental nature of which you are aware during the experience of this space/time continuum is as much of a surface illusion as is the chemical body complex.

Ra 我是 Ra。用你们的话说，心智复合体会遭受重大损失，因为你们在空间/时间连续体体验中所觉知的大部分心智活动，与化学身体复合体一样，都是一种表面幻象。

In other terms, nothing whatever of importance is lost; the character or, shall we say, pure distillation of emotions and biases, or distortions and wisdoms, if you will, becoming obvious for the first time, shall we say; these pure emotions and wisdoms and bias-distortions being, for the most part, either ignored or underestimated during physical life experience.

用另一种话说，没有任何重要之物丢失；性格，或容我们说，情感与偏好——或变貌与智慧——的纯粹蒸馏，可以说是第一次变得明显；这些纯粹的情感、智慧和偏见变貌，在物理生命体验中大部分被忽视或低估了。

In terms of the spiritual, this channel is then much opened due to the lack of necessity for the forgetting characteristic of third density.

就灵性而言，这个管道此时会大大敞开，因为不再需要第三密度的遗忘特性。

## (30.5)

(30.5) Questioner I would like to know how the mind/body/spirit complexes originate. How, going back as far as necessary, does the— Do they originate by spirit forming mind and mind forming body? Can you tell me this?

(30.5) 发问者 我想知道心/身/灵复合体是如何起源的。追溯到尽可能远的地方，它们是如何——它们是灵性形成心智、心智形成身体这样起源的吗？你能告诉我吗？

Ra I am Ra. We ask you to consider that you are attempting to trace evolution. This evolution is as we have previously described:[48] the consciousness being first, in first density, without movement, a random thing. Whether you may call this mind or body complex is a semantic problem. We call it mind/body complex, recognizing always that in the simplest iota of this complex exists, in its entirety, the One Infinite Creator.

Ra 我是 Ra。我们请你考虑，你正在尝试追溯进化。这种进化正如我们之前所描述的：[48] 意识首先出现，在第一密度中，没有运动，是一种随机之物。你将其称为心智复合体还是身体复合体，是一个语义问题。我们称之为心/身复合体，始终认识到，在这个复合体最简单的微点中，就完整地存在着太一无限造物主。

This mind/body complex then in second density discovering the growing and turning towards the light, thus awakening what you may call the spirit complex, that which intensifies the upward spiraling towards the love and light of the Infinite Creator.

这个心/身复合体随后在第二密度中发现了成长并转向光，从而唤醒了你们可称之为灵性复合体的东西，它强化了朝向无限造物主之爱与光的向上螺旋。

The addition of this spirit complex, though apparent rather than real, it having existed potentially from the beginning of space/time, perfects itself by graduation into third density. When the mind/body/spirit complex becomes aware of the possibility of service to self or other-self, then the mind/body/spirit complex is activated.

这个灵性复合体的加入，尽管表面而非真实——它从空间/时间之初就潜在地存在了——通过毕业进入第三密度而完善自身。当心/身/灵复合体意识到服务自我或服务他人的可能性时，心/身/灵复合体就被激活了。

我是 Ra。你们做得很好，我的朋友们。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中离开你们。那么，前行吧，在太一造物主的大能与和平中欢欣鼓舞。Adonai。
