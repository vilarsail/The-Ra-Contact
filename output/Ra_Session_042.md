## (42.2)

(42.2) Questioner I will just read it very rapidly, the question, then.
(42.2) 发问者 那我就快速读一下这个问题。

I am going to make a statement and ask you to comment on its degree of accuracy. I am assuming that the balanced entity would not be swayed either towards positive or negative emotions by any situation which he might confront. By remaining unemotional in any situation, the balanced entity may clearly discern the appropriate and necessary responses in harmony with the Law of One for each situation.
我将陈述一个观点，并请你们评论其准确程度。我假设一个平衡的实体不会被他可能面对的任何情境摆向正面或负面情绪。通过对任何情境都保持无情绪，平衡的实体可以清晰地辨别出与每一个情境中一的法则相和谐的适当且必要的回应。

Most entities on our planet find themselves unconsciously caught up into every emotional situation which they come in contact with according to their own unique biases. And, because of these biases, they are unable to see clearly teach/learning opportunities and appropriate response in each emotional situation and must, therefore, through a process of much trial and error and enduring of resulting pain, repeat such situations many, many times until they become consciously aware of the need to balance their energy centers and thusly their responses and behaviors.
我们星球上的大多数实体发现自己依照各自独特的偏见，无意识地陷入他们所接触的每一个情感情境中。并且，由于这些偏见，他们无法清晰地看见每个情感情境中的教导/学习机会与适当回应，因此必须通过大量试错和承受随之而来的痛苦的过程，一次又一次地重复这些情境，直到他们变得有意识地觉察到需要平衡自己的能量中心，从而平衡他们的回应和行为。

Once a person becomes consciously aware of the need to balance their energy centers and responses, the next step is to allow the appropriately positive or negative responses to emotional situations to flow smoothly through their being without retaining any of the emotional coloration after it has been consciously observed and allowed to flow through the being. And I am assuming that this ability to consciously observe the positively or negatively charged energy flowing through the being may be augmented by practice of the balancing exercises you have given us, with the result in balance being achieved for the entity which would allow him to remain unemotional and undistorted in regards to the Law of One in any situation, much like the objective viewer of the television movie.
一旦一个人变得有意识地觉察到需要平衡他们的能量中心和回应，下一步就是允许对情感情境的适当正面或负面回应顺畅地流过他们的存有，在其被有意识地观察并允许流过存有之后，不保留任何情绪色彩。并且我假设，这种有意识地观察流过存有的正面或负面充能的能力，可以通过练习你给予我们的平衡练习来增强，其结果是该实体达成平衡，这会使他在任何情境中就一的法则而言保持无情绪和无扭曲，很像电视电影的客观观看者。

Is this correct?
这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This is an incorrect application of the balancing which we have discussed.[65] The exercise of first experiencing feelings and then consciously discovering their antitheses within the being has as its objective not the smooth flow of feelings, both positive and negative, while remaining unswayed, but rather the objective of becoming unswayed. This is a simpler result and takes much practice, shall we say.
Ra 我是 Ra。这是对我们所讨论过的平衡的一种不正确应用。[65] 首先体验感觉，然后有意识地在存有内发现它们的对立面的练习，其目标并非让正面和负面感觉顺畅流动，同时保持不为所动，而是变得不为所动的目标。容我们说，这是一个更简单的结果，需要大量练习。

The catalyst of experience works in order for the learn/teachings of this density to occur. However, if there is seen in the being a response, even if it is simply observed, the entity is still using the catalyst for learn/teaching. The end result is that the catalyst is no longer needed. Thus this density is no longer needed.
体验的催化剂起作用是为了让这个密度的学习/教导得以发生。然而，如果在存有中看到一个回应，即使它只是被观察，该实体仍在利用催化剂进行学习/教导。最终结果是催化剂不再被需要。因此这个密度也不再被需要。

This is not indifference or objectivity but a finely tuned compassion and love which sees all things as love. This seeing elicits no response due to catalytic reactions. Thus the entity is now able to become co-Creator of experiential occurrences. This is the truer balance.
这不是冷漠或客观，而是一种精细调谐的悲悯与爱，它将所有事物视为爱。这种看见不会因催化反应而引发回应。于是该实体现在能够成为体验性事件的共同造物主。这是更真实的平衡。

如果一只动物，容我说一头在围栏里的公牛，因为你误入它的围栏而攻击你，你会迅速躲开它的路线，但你不会责怪它。或者，你除了害怕它可能伤害你之外，不会有太多情绪反应。

However, if you encounter another self in his territory and he attacks you, your response may be more of an emotional nature creating physical bodily responses. Am I correct in assuming that when your response to the animal and to the other-self, seeing both as the Creator, and loving both, and understanding their action in attacking you is the action of their free will, then you have balanced yourself correctly in this area? 然而，如果你在另一个自我的领地上遇到他，而他攻击你，你的回应可能更具情绪性，产生身体上的生理反应。我假设，当你对动物和另一个自我的回应，都是将两者视为造物主，并爱两者，并理解他们攻击你的行为是他们的自由意志的行为，那么你在这方面就正确地平衡了自己，这个假设是否正确？这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This is basically correct. However, the balanced entity will see in the seeming attack of an other-self the causes of this action which are, in most cases, of a more complex nature than the cause of the attack of the second-density bull, as was your example. Thus, this balanced entity would be open to many more opportunities for service to a third-density other-self.
Ra 我是 Ra。这基本正确。然而，平衡的实体将在一个其他自我的看似攻击中看到这个行为的原因，在大多数情况下，这些原因比你的例子中第二密度公牛的攻击原因更为复杂。因此，这个平衡的实体将向一个第三密度的其他自我敞开更多服务的机会。
