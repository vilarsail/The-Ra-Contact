# § Ra Session_009


January 27, 1981
1981年1月27日

## (9.0)

(9.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and the light of our Infinite Creator. We communicate now.
(9.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在我们无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (9.1)

(9.1) Questioner We are definitely going to make the Law of One the primary portion of this book. I apologize for getting sidetracked on these subjects. We're in the position of, shall we say, beating around as to what direction to go with the book to begin with. For this reason I have asked a few questions, and probably will ask a few more questions in the early part of these sessions, that will be somewhat meaningless with respect to application of the Law of One because my own ignorance of what I'm doing. However, I expect to become more proficient rapidly while we go on.
(9.1) 发问者 我们肯定打算将一的法则作为本书的主要部分。我很抱歉在这些话题上偏离了方向。我们处于——可以说——在刚开始时对这本书的方向有些摸索的状态。因此，我在这些集会的早期部分问了一些问题，可能还会再问几个问题，这些问题就一的法则的应用而言，将有些无关紧要，因为我自己不知道自己在做什么。然而，随着我们继续，我期望能迅速变得更加熟练。

There are a couple of questions that are probably meaningless, but if I could get them out of the way . . . they're bothering me a little bit.
有几个问题可能毫无意义，但如果我能把它们处理掉……它们让我有点困扰。

Is it possible for you to suggest a publisher for this book?
你们能否为这本书推荐一个出版商？

Ra I am Ra. No.
Ra 我是 Ra。不能。

## (9.2)

(9.2) Questioner Is it possible for you to tell us of anything in our past incarnations, our past experiences before this incarnation?
(9.2) 发问者 你们能否告诉我们一些关于我们过去转世的事情，也就是我们在这次转世之前的经历？

Ra I am Ra. It is possible. However, such information as this is carefully guarded by your mind/body/spirit being totality so that your present space/time experiences will be undiluted.
Ra 我是 Ra。这是可能的。然而，这类信息被你们的心/身/灵全体的存有精心守护着，以便你们当前的空间/时间体验不会被稀释。

Let us scan for harmless material for your beingness. [20-second pause.] I am, in the distortion of desire for your freedom from preconception, able to speak only generally.
让我们扫描一下对你们的存有而言无害的材料。[停顿20秒。] 我，出于对你们免于先入之见的渴望这一变貌，只能泛泛而谈。

There have been several times when this group worked and dwelt together. The relationships varied. There is balanced karma, as you call it; each thus the teacher of each. The work has involved healing, understanding the uses of the earth energy, and work in aid of civilizations which called, just as your sphere has done, and we have come. This ends the material which we consider harmless.
这个群体曾数次一起工作和居住。关系各不相同。存在着你们所称的平衡的业力；因此每个人都是彼此的导师。这些工作涉及疗愈、理解地球能量的运用，以及援助那些发出呼求的文明——正如你们星球所做的那样，我们因此前来。我们视为无害的材料到此为止。

## (9.3)

(9.3) Questioner The healing exercises that you gave to us are of such a nature that it is best to concentrate on a particular exercise at a time. I would like to ask at this time what exercise I should concentrate on. Possibly a little exercise change . . . should concentrate on [inaudible], say tonight?
(9.3) 发问者 你们给我们的疗愈练习的性质是，最好一次专注于一个特定的练习。此时我想问一下我应该专注于哪个练习。也许稍微换一下练习……今晚应该专注于[听不清]？

Ra I am Ra. Again, to direct your judgment is an intrusion upon your space/time-continuum distortion called future. To speak of past or present within our distortion/judgment limits is acceptable. To guide rather than teach/learn is not acceptable to our distortion in regards to teach/learning. We instead can suggest a process whereby each chooses the first of the exercises given in the order in which we gave them, which you, in your discernment, feel is not fully appreciated by your mind/body/spirit complex.
Ra 我是 Ra。再次，指导你们的判断是对你们称为未来的空间/时间连续体变貌的侵扰。在我们的变貌/判断限度内谈论过去或现在是可以接受的。然而，指导而非教导/学习，就教导/学习而言，不符合我们的变貌。相反，我们可以建议一个过程：每个人选择我们按给出顺序所提供练习中的第一个——即你们凭辨识力认为未被你们心/身/灵复合体充分领会的那个练习。

This is the proper choice—building from the foundation, making sure the ground is good for the building. We have assessed for you the intensity of this effort in terms of energy expended. You will take this in mind and be patient, for we have not given a short or easy program of consciousness learn/teaching.
这是正确的选择——从基础开始建造，确保地基适合建筑。我们已经为你们评估了这项努力在能量消耗方面的强度。你们要牢记这一点并保持耐心，因为我们并未给出一个简短或容易的意识学习/教导计划。

## (9.4)

(9.4) Questioner The way I understand the process of evolution of a planetary population is that a population has a certain amount of time to progress. This is generally divided into three 25,000-year cycles. At the end of 75,000 years, the planet progresses itself. What caused this situation to come about? The preciseness of the years, 25,000 years, etc. What set this up to begin with?
(9.4) 发问者 我对行星人口进化过程的理解是，一个种群有一定的时间来进步。这通常分为三个 25,000 年的周期。在 75,000 年结束时，行星自身会进步。是什么导致了这种情况的产生？年份的精确性，25,000 年等等。是什么一开始设立了这些？

Ra I am Ra. Visualize, if you will, the particular energy which, outward flowing and inward coagulating, formed the tiny realm of the creation governed by your Council of Saturn. Continue seeing the rhythm of this process. The living flow creates a rhythm which is as inevitable as one of your timepieces. Each of your planetary entities began the first cycle when the energy nexus was able in that environment to support such mind/body experiences. Thus, each of your planetary entities is on a different cyclical schedule, as you might call it. The timing of these cycles is a measurement equal to a portion of intelligent energy.
Ra 我是 Ra。请观想那特定的能量，它向外流动并向内凝聚，形成了由你们土星议会管辖的、造物中的微小领域。继续观想这过程的韵律。这活生生的流动创造了一种韵律，如同你们的计时器一样不可避免。你们每一个行星实体都在能量核心能够在该环境中支持这类心/身体验时开始了第一个周期。因此，你们的每一个行星实体都处于不同的循环时间表上，正如你们所称呼的那样。这些周期的计时是一种量度，等同于智能能量的一部分。

This intelligent energy offers a type of clock. The cycles move as precisely as a clock strikes your hour. Thus, the gateway from intelligent energy to intelligent infinity opens regardless of circumstance on the striking of the hour.
这种智能能量提供了一种时钟。这些循环的运转如同时钟报时一样精确。因此，从智能能量到智能无限的通道在时钟敲响时，无论何种情况都会开启。

(9.5) 发问者 最初的、这个星球上的第一批实体……他们的起源是什么？他们在来到这个星球之前在哪里？

Ra I am Ra. The first entities upon this planet were water, fire, air and earth.
Ra 我是 Ra。这个星球上的第一批实体是水、火、风和土。

## (9.6)

(9.6) Questioner The people that we now have—the first people, [inaudible] like us—where did they come from? How did they evolve?
(9.6) 发问者 我们现在拥有的人——第一批人，[听不清] 像我们一样——他们从哪里来？他们是如何进化的？

Ra I am Ra. You speak of third-density experience. The first of those to come here were brought from another planet in your solar system called by you the Red Planet, Mars. This planet's environment became inhospitable to third-density beings. The first entities, therefore, were of this race, as you may call it, manipulated somewhat by those who were Guardians at that time.
Ra 我是 Ra。你谈的是第三密度体验。第一批来到这里的实体是从你们太阳系中另一个行星被带来的，你们称之为红色星球，火星。该行星的环境变得不适合第三密度存有居住。因此，第一批实体属于这个种族，正如你们可以称呼的那样，在当时被那些守护者们进行了一定程度的操控。

## (9.7)

(9.7) Questioner What race is that, and how did they get from Mars to here?
(9.7) 发问者 那是什么种族，他们是如何从火星来到这里的？

Ra I am Ra. The race is a combination of the mind/body/spirit complexes of those of your so-called Red Planet and a careful series of genetical adjustments made by the Guardians of that time. These entities arrived, or were preserved, for the experience upon your sphere by a type of birthing which is non-reproductive, but consists of preparing genetic material for the incarnation of the mind/body/spirit complexes of those entities from the Red Planet.
Ra 我是 Ra。该种族是你们所谓红色星球上的那些心/身/灵复合体与当时守护者们进行的一系列精心基因调整的结果。这些实体通过一种非生殖性的诞生方式来到——或说被保存下来——以便在你们星球上体验，这种方式包括为来自红色星球的那些实体的心/身/灵复合体投生准备基因材料。

(9.8) 发问者 那么我假设你们说的是，守护者们在那个种族从我们所知的火星物理层面死亡后，将其转移到了这里。这是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This is correct.
Ra 我是 Ra。这是正确的。

## (9.9)

(9.9) Questioner The Guardians obviously were acting with an understanding of the Law of One in doing this. Can you explain the application of the Law of One in this process?
(9.9) 发问者 守护者们显然是在理解一的法则的情况下这样做的。你能解释一下一的法则在这一过程中的应用吗？

Ra I am Ra. The Law of One was named by these Guardians as the bringing of the wisdom of the Guardians in contact with the entities from the Red Planet, thus melding the social memory complex of the Guardian race and the Red Planet race. It, however, took an increasing amount of distortion into the application of the Law of One from the viewpoint of other Guardians, and it is from this beginning action that the quarantine of this planet was instituted, for it was felt that the free will of those of the Red Planet had been abridged.
Ra 我是 Ra。这些守护者将一的法则命名为：将守护者的智慧与来自红色星球的实体接触，从而融合守护者种族与红色星球种族的社会记忆复合体。然而，从其他守护者的观点来看，这种对一的法则的应用引入了越来越多的变貌，正是从这个初始行动起，这个行星的隔离被建立了起来，因为那时认为红色星球上那些实体的自由意志被缩减了。

## (9.10)

(9.10) Questioner Were the entities of the Red Planet following the Law of One prior to leaving the Red Planet?
(9.10) 发问者 红色星球的实体在离开红色星球之前是否遵循一的法则？

However, the tendencies of these people towards bellicose actions caused such difficulties in the atmospheric environment of their planet that it became inhospitable for third-density experience before the end of its cycle. Thus, the Red Planet entities were unharvested and continued in your illusion to attempt to learn the Law of Love.
Ra 红色星球的实体当时正试图学习爱的法则，这法则构成了一的法则的原初变貌之一。然而，这些人的好战倾向在其行星的大气环境中造成了如此大的困难，以至于该行星在周期结束前就变得不适合第三密度体验。因此，红色星球的实体未被收割，并继续在你们的幻象中尝试学习爱的法则。

另一个种族是由守护者们提供在此密度居住的存有，守护者们希望——在发生你们称为核战争的事件时——为此刻处于这个密度的那些心/身/灵复合体提供经过适当设计的物理载具，正如你们会称呼这些化学复合体那样。
