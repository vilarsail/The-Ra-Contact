# § Ra Session_081


March 22, 1982
1982年3月22日

## (81.0)

(81.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.
(81.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (81.1)

(81.1) Questioner Could you first please tell me the condition of the instrument?
(81.1) 发问者 你能首先告诉我该器皿的状况吗？

Ra I am Ra. The physical complex energy is in deficit at this particular space/time nexus due to prolonged psychic accentuation of pre-existing distortions. The remainder of the energy complex levels are as previously stated.
Ra 我是 Ra。在这个特定的空间/时间连接点，由于对先前存在的变貌进行了长时间的心灵强化，该身体复合体的能量处于亏缺状态。其余能量复合体水平如前所述。

## (81.2)

(81.2) Questioner Is this the reason for the instrument's feeling of uninterrupted weariness?
(81.2) 发问者 这就是该器皿感到持续疲倦的原因吗？

Ra I am Ra. There are portions of your space/time in which this may be said to be symptomatic of the psychic greeting reaction. However, the continual weariness is not due to psychic greeting but is, rather, an inevitable consequence of this contact.
Ra 我是 Ra。在你们空间/时间的某些部分，可以说这是心灵致意反应的症状。然而，持续的疲倦并非由于心灵致意，而是这个接触的必然结果。

## (81.3)

(81.3) Questioner Why is this an inevitable consequence? What is the mechanism of contact creating weariness?
(81.3) 发问者 为什么这是必然结果？接触产生疲倦的机制是什么？

Ra I am Ra. The mechanism creating weariness is that connection betwixt the density wherein this instrument’s mind/body/spirit complex is safely kept during these workings, and the altogether variant density in which the instrument’s physical body complex resides at this space/time. As the instrument takes on more of the coloration of the resting density, the third-density experience seems more heavy and wearisome.
Ra 我是 Ra。产生疲倦的机制是：该器皿的心/身/灵复合体在这些工作期间被安全保存的密度，与该器皿的物理身体复合体在此空间/时间所居住的完全不同的密度之间的连接。随着该器皿呈现出更多休息密度的色彩，第三密度体验显得更加沉重和疲倦。

This was accepted by the instrument, as it desired to be of service. Therefore, we accept also this effect about which nothing of which we are aware may be done.
这被该器皿所接受，因为它渴望服务。因此，我们也接受这个效应，据我们所知，对此无能为力。

## (81.4)

(81.4) Questioner Is the effect a function of the number of sessions, and has it reached a peak level, or will it continue to increase in effect?
(81.4) 发问者 这个效应是会话次数的函数吗？它是否已达到峰值水平，还是会继续增加？

Ra I am Ra. This wearying effect will continue but should not be confused with the physical energy levels having only to do with the, as you would call it, daily round of experience.
Ra 我是 Ra。这种疲倦效应将持续，但不应与物理能量水平混淆，后者仅与你们所谓的日常经验有关。

In this sphere those things which are known already to aid this instrument will continue to be of aid. You will, however, notice the gradual increase in transparency, shall we say, of the vibrations of the instrument.
在这方面，那些已知能帮助该器皿的事物将继续有所帮助。然而，你们将注意到该器皿振动的透明度逐渐增加，容我们说。

## (81.5)

(81.5) 发问者 我不理解你最后那句话的意思。你能解释一下吗？

Ra I am Ra. Weariness of the time/space nature may be seen to be that reaction of transparent or pure vibrations with impure, confused, or opaque environs.
Ra 我是 Ra。时间/空间性质的疲倦可以被视为透明或纯净振动与不纯净、混乱或不透明环境的反应。

## (81.7)

(81.7) Questioner Then we would also experience the uninterrupted wearying effect as a consequence of the contact. Is this correct?
(81.7) 发问者 那么，作为接触的结果，我们也会经历持续疲倦的效应。这是正确的吗？

Ra 我是 Ra。该器皿，由于接触的本质，承受了这个效应的主要冲击。

Each of the support group, by offering the love and the light of the One Infinite Creator in unqualified support in these workings, and in energy transfers for the purpose of these workings, experiences between 10 and 15 percent, roughly, of this effect. It is cumulative and identical in the continual nature of its manifestation.
每个支持小组成员，通过在这些工作中以无条件的支持提供太一无限造物主的爱与光，以及为这些工作的目的进行能量转移，大约承受了这个效应的10%到15%。它是累积性的，在其持续表现的性质上是相同的。

## (81.8)

(81.8) Questioner What could be the result of this continued wearying effect after a long period?
(81.8) 发问者 长期持续这种疲倦效应可能导致什么结果？

Ra I am Ra. You ask a general query with infinite answers. We shall over-generalize in order to attempt to reply.
Ra 我是 Ra。你问了一个具有无限答案的普遍问题。我们将过度概括以尝试回答。

One group might be tempted and thus lose the very contact which caused the difficulty. So the story would end.
一个小组可能受到诱惑，从而失去那个导致困难的联系本身。于是故事就结束了。

Another group might be strong at first but not faithful in the face of difficulty. Thus the story would end.
另一个小组可能起初坚强，但在困难面前不能保持忠诚。于是故事就结束了。

Another group might choose the path of martyrdom in its completeness and use the instrument until its physical body complex failed from the harsh toll demanded when all energy was gone.
另一个小组可能选择完全的殉道之路，使用该器皿直到其物理身体复合体因所有能量耗尽所要求的严酷代价而衰竭。

This particular group, at this particular nexus, is attempting to conserve the vital energy of the instrument. It is attempting to balance love of service and wisdom of service, and it is faithful to the service in the face of difficulty. Temptation has not yet ended this group’s story.
这个特定的小组，在这个特定的连接点，正在尝试保存该器皿的生命能量。它正在尝试平衡服务的爱与服务的智慧，并且在困难面前对服务保持忠诚。诱惑尚未结束这个小组的故事。

We may not know the future, but the probability of this situation continuing over a relatively substantial period of your space/time is large. The significant factor is the will of the instrument and of the group to serve. That is the only cause for balancing the slowly increasing weariness which will continue to distort your perceptions. Without this will the contact might be possible but finally seem too much of an effort.
我们无法知道未来，但这种状况在你们空间/时间的相当长一段时期内持续的可能性很大。重要的因素是该器皿和小组服务的意志。这是平衡那将持续扭曲你们感知的、缓慢增加的疲倦的唯一原因。没有这个意志，接触或许是可能的，但最终会显得太过费力。
