# § Ra Session_056


June 8, 1981
1981年6月8日

## (56.0)

(56.0) Ra I am Ra. I greet you in the love and in the light of the One Infinite Creator. We communicate now.
(56.0) Ra 我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中向你们致意。我们现在开始通讯。

## (56.1)

(56.1) Questioner Would you first please give me an indication of the condition of the instrument?
(56.1) 发问者 你能首先告诉我器皿状况的指示吗？

Ra I am Ra. This instrument is severely distorted towards weakness of the mental and physical complexes at this time, and is under psychic attack due to this opportunity.
Ra 我是 Ra。这个器皿此时严重地朝向心智和身体复合体的虚弱而变貌，并且由于这个时机而处于 psychic 攻击之下。

## (56.2)

(56.2) Questioner Would it be better to discontinue the contact at this time?
(56.2) 发问者 此时中止接触是否更好？

Ra I am Ra. This is entirely at your discretion. This instrument has some energy transferred which is available. However, it is not great due to the effects as previously stated.
Ra 我是 Ra。这完全由你们自行决定。这个器皿有一些可用的转移能量。然而，由于先前所述的影响，它并不大。

We, if you desire to question us further at this working, will as always attempt to safeguard this instrument. We feel that you are aware of the parameters without further elaboration.
如果你们希望在此次工作中进一步向我们提问，我们将一如既往地尝试保护这个器皿。我们觉得你们知晓这些参数，无需进一步说明。

## (56.3)

(56.3) Questioner In that case, I will ask how does the pyramid shape work?
(56.3) 发问者 那样的话，我会问金字塔形状是如何运作的？

Ra I am Ra. We are assuming that you wish to know the principle of the shapes, angles, and intersections of the pyramid at what you call Giza.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们假设你想知道你们称为吉萨的金字塔的形状、角度和交点的原理。

In reality, the pyramid shape does no work. It does not work. It is an arrangement for the centralization, as well as the diffraction, of the spiraling upward light energy as it is being used by the mind/body/spirit complex.
实际上，金字塔形状不做功。它不运作。它是一种安排，用于螺旋上升的光能量在被心/身/灵复合体使用时的集中化以及衍射。

The spiraling nature of light is such that the magnetic fields of an individual are affected by spiraling energy. Certain shapes offer an echo chamber, shall we say, or an intensifier for spiraling prana, as some have called this all-present, primal distortion of the One Infinite Creator.
光的螺旋性质是这样的：个体的磁场受到螺旋能量的影响。某些形状提供了一个回声室，我们可以说，或者一个螺旋普拉纳的增强器，如某些人称呼这个太一无限造物主之遍在的、原初的变貌。

If the intent is to intensify the necessity for the entity’s own will to call forth the inner light in order to match the intensification of the spiraling light energy, the entity will be placed in what you have called the Queen’s Chamber position in this particular shaped object. This is the initiatory place and is the place of resurrection.
如果意图是强化实体自身意志召唤内在之光的必要性，以便匹配螺旋光能量的增强，实体将被放置在这个特定形状物体中你们称为王后室的位置。这是启蒙之地，也是复活之地。

The off-set place, representing the spiral as it is in motion, is the appropriate position for one to be healed; as in this position an entity’s vibratory magnetic nexi are interrupted in their normal flux. Thus a possibility/probability vortex ensues: a new beginning, shall we say, is offered for the entity in which the entity may choose a less distorted, weak, or blocked configuration of energy-center-magnetic distortions.
偏移位置，代表处于运动中的螺旋，是一个要被疗愈者的适当位置；因为在这个位置，一个实体的振动磁性联结点在其正常流动中被中断。因此一个可能性/概率性漩涡随之而来：一个新的开始，我们可以说，被提供给该实体，在其中实体可以选择一个较少变貌、虚弱或阻塞的能量中心磁性变貌配置。

The function of the healer and crystal may not be over-emphasized, for this power of interruption must needs be controlled, shall we say, with incarnate intelligence; the intelligence being that of one which recognizes energy patterns; which, without judging, recognizes blockage, weakness, and other distortion; and which is capable of visualizing, through the regularity of self and of crystal, the less distorted other-self to be healed.
疗愈者和水晶的功能再怎么强调也不为过，因为这种中断的力量必须被——我们可以说——投生智能所控制；这种智能是那种识别能量模式的智能；它不评判地识别阻塞、虚弱和其他变貌；并能够通过自我和水晶的规律性，观想那个要被疗愈的较少变貌的其他自我。

Other shapes which are arched, groined, vaulted, conical, or as your tepees are also shapes with this type of intensification of spiraling light. Your caves, being rounded, are places of power due to this shaping.
其他形状，如拱形、交叉拱顶、穹顶、圆锥形，或如你们的圆锥帐篷，也是具有这种螺旋光增强类型的形状。你们的洞穴，由于是圆形的，是因此塑造而具有力量的地方。

It is to be noted that these shapes are dangerous. We are quite pleased to have the opportunity to enlarge upon the subject of shapes such as the pyramid, for we wish, as part of our honor/duty, to state that there are many wrong uses for these curved shapes; for with improper placement, improper intentions, or lack of the crystallized being functioning as channel for healing, the sensitive entity will be distorted more rather than less in some cases.
需要注意的是，这些形状是危险的。我们非常高兴有机会扩展关于金字塔等形状的主题，因为我们希望，作为我们荣耀/职责的一部分，声明这些弯曲形状有许多错误用途；因为如果放置不当、意图不当，或缺乏结晶化的存有作为疗愈管道来运作，敏感的实体在某些情况下将被更多地变貌，而非更少。

It is to be noted that your peoples build, for the most part, the cornered or square habitations, for they do not concentrate power. It is further to be noted that the spiritual seeker has, for many of your time periods of years, sought the rounded, arched, and peaked forms as an expression of the power of the Creator.[89]
需要注意的是，你们的人民大部分建造有角或方形的居所，因为它们不集中力量。进一步需要注意的是，灵性寻求者在你们许多年时间段中，一直寻求圆形、拱形和尖顶形式，作为造物主力量的一种表达。[89]

## (56.4)

(56.4) Questioner Is there an apex angle that is the angle for maximum efficiency in the pyramid?
(56.4) 发问者 是否存在一个顶点角度，是金字塔中最大效率的角度？

Ra I am Ra. Again, to conserve this instrument’s energy, I am assuming that you intend to indicate the most appropriate angle of apex for healing work. If the shape is such that it is large enough to contain an individual mind/body/spirit complex at the appropriate off-set position within it, the 76° 18′, approximate, angle is useful and appropriate. If the position varies, the angle may vary. Further, if the healer has the ability to perceive distortions with enough discrimination, the position within any pyramid shape may be moved about until results are effected.
Ra 我是 Ra。再次，为了保存这个器皿的能量，我假设你意在指出疗愈工作最合适的顶点角度。如果形状足够大，能够在其中适当的偏移位置容纳一个个体心/身/灵复合体，则大约 76°18′ 的角度是有用且合适的。如果位置变化，角度可能变化。此外，如果疗愈者有能力以足够的辨别力感知变貌，在任何金字塔形状内的位置都可以被移动，直到产生效果。

However, we found this particular angle to be useful. Other social memory complexes, or portions thereof, have determined different apex angles for different uses, not having to do with healing but with learning. When one works with the cone or, shall we say, the silo type of shape, the energy for healing may be found to be in a general circular pattern unique to each shape as a function of its particular height and width and, in the cone shape, the angle of apex. In these cases there are no corner angles. Thus the spiraling energy works in circular motion.
然而，我们发现这个特定角度是有用的。其他社会记忆复合体，或其部分，已经为不同用途确定了不同的顶点角度，这些用途与疗愈无关，而是与学习有关。当一个人使用圆锥体，或我们可以说，筒仓类型的形状工作时，疗愈能量可能被发现处于一个一般的圆形模式中，该模式作为其特定高度和宽度的函数而独特于每个形状，并且在圆锥形状中，取决于顶点角度。在这些情况下，没有角的角度。因此螺旋能量以圆周运动运作。

Ra I am Ra. We can correct this statement.
Ra 我是 Ra。我们可以纠正这个陈述。

## (56.6)

(56.6) Questioner Would you please do that?
(56.6) 发问者 你能那样做吗？

Ra The spiraling energy is beginning to be diffused at the point where it goes through the King’s Chamber position. However—although the spirals continue to intersect, closing and opening in double spiral fashion through the apex angle—the diffusion or strength of the spiraling energies, red through violet color values, lessens if we speak of strength, and gains if we speak of diffusion until at the peak of the pyramid you have a very weak color resolution useful for healing purposes.
Ra 螺旋能量在穿过国王室位置的点开始扩散。然而——尽管螺旋继续交叉，以双螺旋方式通过顶点角度闭合和打开——螺旋能量（红到紫颜色值）的扩散或强度，如果我们谈论强度则减弱，如果我们谈论扩散则增强，直到在金字塔的顶点，你有一个非常微弱的颜色分辨率，对疗愈目的有用。

Thus the King’s Chamber position is chosen as the first spiral after the centered beginning through the Queen’s Chamber position. You may visualize the diffusion angle as the opposite of the pyramid angle, but the angle being less wide than the apex angle of the pyramid, being somewhere between 33 and 54°, depending upon the various rhythms of the planet itself.
因此，国王室位置被选为经过王后室位置的中心化起点之后的第一个螺旋。你可以将扩散角度想象为金字塔角度的相反，但该角度比金字塔的顶点角度更窄，大约在 33° 到 54° 之间，取决于行星本身的各种韵律。

## (56.7)

(56.7) Questioner Then I assume if I start my angle at the bottom of the Queen’s Chamber, and make a 33 to 54° angle from that point, so that half of that angle falls on the side of the centerline the King’s Chamber is on, that will indicate the diffusion of the spectrum, starting from the point at the bottom of the Queen’s Chamber; let’s say if we were using a 40° angle, we would have a 20° diffusion to the left of the centerline, passing through the King’s Chamber. Is that correct?
(56.7) 发问者 那么我假设，如果我从王后室的底部开始我的角度，并从那个点做一个 33° 到 54° 的角度，使得该角度的一半落在国王室所在的中线一侧，那将指示光谱的扩散，从王后室底部的点开始；假设我们使用 40° 的角度，我们将在中线左侧有 20° 的扩散，穿过国王室。那是否正确？

Ra I am Ra. This will be the last full question of this session.
Ra 我是 Ra。这将是这次集会的最后一个完整问题。

It is correct that half of the aforementioned angle passes through the King’s Chamber position. It is incorrect to assume that the Queen’s Chamber is the foundation of the angle. The angle will begin somewhere between the Queen’s Chamber position and thence downward towards the level of the resonating chamber, off-set for the healing work.
上述角度的一半穿过国王室位置是正确的。假设王后室是该角度的基础是不正确的。该角度将开始于王后室位置和从那里向下朝向共振室水平之间的某处，为疗愈工作而偏移。

This variation is dependent upon various magnetic fluxes of the planet. The King’s Chamber position is designed to intersect the strongest spiral of the energy flow regardless of where the angle begins. However, as it passes through the Queen Chamber position this spiraling energy is always centered and at its strongest point.
这种变化取决于行星的各种磁通量。国王室位置被设计为与能量流的最强螺旋交叉，无论角度从哪里开始。然而，当它穿过王后室位置时，这种螺旋能量始终是中心化的，并且处于其最强点。

May we answer any brief queries at this time?
此时我们可以回答任何简短的问题吗？

我是 Ra。我在太一无限造物主的爱与光中离开你们。因此，向前去吧，在太一无限造物主的力量与和平中欢欣鼓舞。Adonai。
